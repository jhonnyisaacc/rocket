## Simple baselines, timing, and limits

- **“The label does not persist.”** Both earlier `WATCH_ENTER` rows were below the floor and `AVOID` on the later vault read (2/2). That simple baseline fits these two rows. No positive persistence or edge is shown.
- **“The mass of vault prints stays under $1,000.”** The later cohort had 12/19 finite prints under $1,000, and a $343.80 median. The two large outliers show why the baseline describes a majority, not every mint.
- The CLI snapshot, later account reads, forward reads, and Dex check have separate clocks. The initial unkeyed CLI scan cannot be relabeled as a Helius-confirmed point-in-time scan. A connected Helius account read confirmed mint existence, while the raw reserve measurements came from the public Solana RPC. Public RPC JSON-RPC batching failed; `getMultipleAccounts` succeeded. Neither source failure nor the NOVA null was filled with a guessed value.
- Slippage, fees, executable size, order failure, and fills were **not measured**. No order was placed and no public executable quote was captured. The vault formula is a pool-state measure, not a fill price.

## Reassessment

The cent-match claim failed in this held-out check, the strict newest-page wording needs narrowing, and the two observed `WATCH_ENTER` labels did not persist. Turnover and a low-median/right-tail distribution survived only as descriptions of this bounded intake and ranking rule. None is a repeatable point-in-time trading edge. **Phase E is not warranted.** Stop after this Phase D pass. Any resumed research remains Phase D and would need a new, prospectively timed cohort with Helius available to the CLI, fixed forward read times, and an observable executable quote before an edge claim could be tested. `edge` remains `NO_EDGE_VALIDATED`; `execution_enabled` remains false.
