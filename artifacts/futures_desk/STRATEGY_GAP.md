## What the table says

Of the ten pieces, none is both documented and live. PR #29 made the book visible; it did not implement the documented price theory. Its `ZONE` is "agreed close-to-close signs + inside 50–86% of a rolling 4h swing", which is a different object from the documented entry zone (75–86% of a locked impulse, inside a ≥3-factor confluence zone, with a 4h reaction). Counting 1,284 `ZONE` rows is not evidence against PR #28's zero admissions, and it is not a reason to relax them.
