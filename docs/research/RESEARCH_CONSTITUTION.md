# Rocket research constitution

Status: authoritative research process, 2026-09-24. This applies to research branches until superseded by a reviewed change.

## Authority and purpose

The hierarchy is **Research Constitution → domain Pillar → current Frontier → experiment, component and data-truth ledgers → historical material**. A historical report records evidence; its old “next step” does not control current work. The aim is a reproducible, economically meaningful Rocket decision contract. A plausible narrative or retrospective classifier is not sufficient.

## Method

Record each question, mechanism, cheap falsifier, baseline, frozen feature/label/evaluation definition, dataset fingerprint and code revision before examining results. Separate discovery from temporal out-of-sample validation and untouched final holdout. Count material trials. If labels overlap, use a split that prevents cross-boundary leakage. Include the available negative universe and missing-data cases. Ablate each added feature family against a simpler baseline.

Evaluate point-in-time availability, protocol truth, calibration, base rates, ranking and realized economic outcomes after costs. Report uncertainty, regime and cohort breakdowns. Model failure, latency, impact and exit coverage. A classification metric alone cannot promote a strategy. The decision state remains research-only until a frozen policy has passed retrospective and prospective checks.

Every durable conclusion uses `KNOWN`, `OPEN`, `CANDIDATE` or `REJECTED` and records what evidence shows, what it does not show, why it is believed, and a reopening condition. Reopen `REJECTED` only for new independent data, a method/data bug, a materially different mechanism, a transfer/replication test or explicit operator decision. Do not search thresholds to rescue a failed result.

Component promotion: `UNTESTED → DISCOVERY_SIGNAL → OOS_CANDIDATE → ROBUST_COMPONENT`. Strategy promotion: `RESEARCH → CANDIDATE → FROZEN → SHADOW → ACCEPTED`. A changed policy receives a new version. Freeze and record all prospective decisions, including abstentions and failures, before resolving outcomes. Execution remains disabled until separately authorized.
