# Futures frontier

Status: `OPEN`, one active question, 2026-09-24.

**Question:** Can a causal crypto-perpetual directional mechanism show stable gross information across periods, then retain useful expectancy after funding, turnover and intended-venue execution costs?

The frozen [FUT-001 2025 OOS cheap gate](experiments/FUT-001-OOS.md) is the first answer for one portfolio construction: multi-horizon 20/60/120-day trend had a small, uncertain positive arithmetic mean at 20bps, negative compounding, and a negative mean at 40bps across predeclared settlement bounds. It is cost-sensitive and not promoted. The 60-day control's stronger one-year point estimate has a wide interval crossing zero and negative discovery performance; it is not a selected winner. The prior rejected 50-close breakout remains a separate exact contract. No result validates trend broadly or Hyperliquid execution.

The [FUT-002 earlier-year replication](experiments/FUT-002-RESULT.md) tested that selected 60-day control without changing its weights or costs. It earned a strong but first-quarter-heavy 2021 result and a negative 2022 net mean across the frozen settlement stress, failing the predeclared both-years check. This closes the 60-day control as a current successor; no strategy has been promoted.

The frozen [FUT-003 Bybit venue-transfer diagnostic](experiments/FUT-003-RESULT.md) then applied the unchanged multi-horizon rule to a second venue's 2022 tape. Its gross statistic was weakly positive and 20bps mean barely positive, but 40bps mean negative under every settlement bound, with negative 20bps compounding and a wide month-block interval. The old trend baseline remains cost-sensitive across two venues and is closed as the current strategy candidate.

FUT-001's multi-horizon gross predictive statistic was near zero in 2023–2024 and only small positive in 2025. The selected 60-day control had strong first-quarter 2021 gross returns, nearly flat 2022 gross returns, negative 2023–2024 gross returns, and a positive 2025 point estimate; [FUT-002](experiments/FUT-002-RESULT.md) failed its 2022 net falsifier even with optimistic settlement bounds. These inspected outcomes motivated a distinct gross-information trial without selecting a 2025 side, coin, quarter or nearby trend horizon.

The [spot/perpetual basis source audit](BASIS_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) verified 13,947 spot monthly ZIPs, repaired one malformed KLAY month with independent daily sources while leaving its corrupt October 30 record missing, and established return-free dual-market eligibility. The [FUT-004 factor](experiments/FUT-004.md) then froze high-basis-long/low-basis-short quartiles before outcomes. Its [pre-result data amendment](experiments/FUT-004-DATA.md) replaced an unrepaired futures archive gap with the already checksum-repaired FUT-002 tape and bounded seven selected closures without changing the rule.

The [FUT-004 discovery result](experiments/FUT-004-RESULT.md) failed the frozen gross-information gate in every settlement scenario: 2022 mean daily gross was −0.03985% to −0.03609%, 2023 was approximately flat, and the combined month-block interval crossed zero. It did not reach a net-cost, 2024 or 2025 score. This closes the exact one-day directional basis factor, not every basis or funding-carry mechanism.

**Immediate discriminating question:** Which independently motivated source of directional information is measurable with point-in-time data and an untouched evaluation chronology? Funding and open interest may describe positioning pressure, but simple sign inversion of FUT-004, a selected asset/quarter, or a nearby trend horizon is not a new mechanism. Audit historical source availability and publication timing before freezing another trial. The [Bybit source audit](INDEPENDENT_VENUE_DATA.md) supplies a second venue's 2022 perp tape, but that year has already been inspected for trend and cannot be called a fresh market-regime holdout. A separate [Hyperliquid carry source check](CARRY_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) found historical BTC/ETH funding and daily candles in four fixed 2024/2025 samples. Cross-venue carry has a structural funding payoff but requires a two-venue hedge and contract-level accounting; it is a product-scope candidate, not directional alpha or an inspected strategy return. Any survivor needs venue-specific cessation and cost rules, Hyperliquid overlap, prospective shadow decisions, and a realistic delayed-fill test. Keep the 2026 Binance final holdout untouched and production `rocket crypto scan --json` unchanged while no validated `ENTER_*` contract exists.

Binance's historical OI endpoint is documented as recent-only. Bybit OI measures both sides together, so source feasibility alone does not define a directional forecast. Next inspect full row coverage and timestamp semantics alongside a separately justified positioning mechanism before registering another strategy trial.
