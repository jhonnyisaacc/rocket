# Futures frontier

Status: `OPEN`, one active question, 2026-09-24.

**Question:** Can a causal crypto-perpetual directional mechanism show stable gross information across periods, then retain useful expectancy after funding, turnover and intended-venue execution costs?

The frozen [FUT-001 2025 OOS cheap gate](experiments/FUT-001-OOS.md) is the first answer for one portfolio construction: multi-horizon 20/60/120-day trend had a small, uncertain positive arithmetic mean at 20bps, negative compounding, and a negative mean at 40bps across predeclared settlement bounds. It is cost-sensitive and not promoted. The 60-day control's stronger one-year point estimate has a wide interval crossing zero and negative discovery performance; it is not a selected winner. The prior rejected 50-close breakout remains a separate exact contract. No result validates trend broadly or Hyperliquid execution.

The [FUT-002 earlier-year replication](experiments/FUT-002-RESULT.md) tested that selected 60-day control without changing its weights or costs. It earned a strong but first-quarter-heavy 2021 result and a negative 2022 net mean across the frozen settlement stress, failing the predeclared both-years check. This closes the 60-day control as a current successor; no strategy has been promoted.

The frozen [FUT-003 Bybit venue-transfer diagnostic](experiments/FUT-003-RESULT.md) then applied the unchanged multi-horizon rule to a second venue's 2022 tape. Its gross statistic was weakly positive and 20bps mean barely positive, but 40bps mean negative under every settlement bound, with negative 20bps compounding and a wide month-block interval. The old trend baseline remains cost-sensitive across two venues and is closed as the current strategy candidate.

FUT-001's multi-horizon gross predictive statistic was near zero in 2023–2024 and only small positive in 2025. The selected 60-day control had strong first-quarter 2021 gross returns, nearly flat 2022 gross returns, negative 2023–2024 gross returns, and a positive 2025 point estimate; [FUT-002](experiments/FUT-002-RESULT.md) failed its 2022 net falsifier even with optimistic settlement bounds. These inspected outcomes motivated a distinct gross-information trial without selecting a 2025 side, coin, quarter or nearby trend horizon.

The [spot/perpetual basis source audit](BASIS_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) verified 13,947 spot monthly ZIPs, repaired one malformed KLAY month with independent daily sources while leaving its corrupt October 30 record missing, and established return-free dual-market eligibility. The [FUT-004 factor](experiments/FUT-004.md) then froze high-basis-long/low-basis-short quartiles before outcomes. Its [pre-result data amendment](experiments/FUT-004-DATA.md) replaced an unrepaired futures archive gap with the already checksum-repaired FUT-002 tape and bounded seven selected closures without changing the rule.

The [FUT-004 discovery result](experiments/FUT-004-RESULT.md) failed the frozen gross-information gate in every settlement scenario: 2022 mean daily gross was −0.03985% to −0.03609%, 2023 was approximately flat, and the combined month-block interval crossed zero. It did not reach a net-cost, 2024 or 2025 score. This closes the exact one-day directional basis factor, not every basis or funding-carry mechanism.

The [frozen FUT-005 CME BTC noncommercial positioning-change rule](experiments/FUT-005-RESULT.md) failed its cheap gate in 2023: daily gross and 20bps net means were negative. Its 2024 arithmetic net was positive but compounding was negative and Q1 dominated. Conditional 2025 COT outcomes remain unread. No sign inversion, subgroup, release-lag or threshold rescue is allowed under FUT-005.

The [FUT-006 post-study order-flow result](experiments/FUT-006-RESULT.md) applied a frozen delayed-entry, nonoverlapping twelve-hour BTC/ETH rule to checksum-verified November–December 2024 aggregate trades. Its pooled gross mean was −2.5332 bp and 5 bp-per-side net mean −7.4381 bp per interval; the 10 bp-per-side stress was also negative. BTC gross was negative while ETH gross was positive, and the daily-block interval was wide. The exact rule failed its gross gate before any conditional 2025 replication. Do not select ETH, November, an inverted sign, new threshold or clock from this result. The 2026 final holdout remains untouched.

The [FUT-007 ETH lower-tail funding transfer](experiments/FUT-007-RESULT.md) froze the published BTC sign and 180-day relative decile before ETH outcomes. The 2023H2 window had only 13 signals against its 15-event minimum. The 2024 window had 52 signals, but its mean triggered gross and costed scheduled-day return were negative, failing the gate independently. The positive but sparse 2023 half-year does not justify a 2025 score, and an absolute-negative-rate filter would be a new post-result rule. The cited carry arbitrage requires a spot hedge and does not map to the current one-direction scan contract. The 2026 final holdout remains untouched.

The [FUT-008 near-touch pressure pilot](experiments/FUT-008-RESULT.md) has now failed on both fixed OKX days. Its 107/116 signals cleared sample size, but triggered-minus-all-short gross was negative on each day, and optimistic displayed-touch fills lost −10.286/−9.310 bp per triggered interval at 5 bp per side. Combined 10 bp/side stress lost −19.778 bp. The exact five-minute taker short is closed; 2025 pressure-conditioned outcomes remain unread. The published passive-buy toxicity association concerns a different execution use, so it does not supply a standalone directional edge here.

The [FUT-009 post-study 2024 result](experiments/FUT-009-RESULT.md) failed with 209 active positions: mean price gross −8.229 bp, 16.406 bp below same-date always-long, and scheduled net −6.403/−8.574 bp at 5/10 bp per side. Sample size passed, but gross, incremental and cost gates failed independently. The exact sparse delayed dollar forecast is closed; conditional 2025 outcomes remain unread. The source paper's spot VAR is not replicated by this result.

**Immediate discriminating question:** Can **identified loss-realizing or forced-close flow on Hyperliquid** yield a causal delayed directional reversal large enough to clear the intended venue's taker fee, spread and impact? A [primary wallet-level study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7270041) reports that price impact after front-end traders' loss-realizing closes reverses past zero, but its public abstract gives no standalone net rule or costed effect size. [Hyperliquid's historical-data documentation](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/historical-data) identifies node-fills archives and L2 snapshots; the archives are requester-pays and may have gaps. Before registering another trade trial, check whether fill `closedPnl`, liquidation flags, timestamps and wallet attribution can be used at decision time; estimate data-transfer and execution hurdles and a cheap source/economic falsifier. Do not infer a tradable edge from the paper's aggregate retail losses, which include fees paid to the venue. Keep production `rocket crypto scan --json` unchanged while no validated contract exists.

The [Bybit source audit](INDEPENDENT_VENUE_DATA.md) supplies a second venue's 2022 perp tape, but that year has already been inspected for trend and cannot be called a fresh market-regime holdout. A separate [Hyperliquid carry source check](CARRY_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) has full 2024–2025 BTC/ETH daily candles and all but one shared hourly funding slot. Cross-venue carry has a structural funding payoff but requires a two-venue hedge and contract-level accounting; its data-coverage gate is mostly resolved, while economic viability and product fit remain open. It is not a directional signal or an inspected strategy return.

The [open-interest source scout](POSITIONING_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) verified small Bybit daily windows in 2022, 2024 and 2025, including a delisted symbol whose later OI rows are zero. Binance's historical OI endpoint is documented as recent-only. Bybit OI measures both sides together, so source feasibility alone does not define a directional forecast. Next inspect full row coverage and timestamp semantics alongside a separately justified positioning mechanism before registering another strategy trial.
