# Futures component ledger

Component states concern incremental value in the current architecture. Historical failures of a whole strategy do not automatically reject a component.

| Component | Role | Status | Evidence / next discriminating test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-horizon time-series trend | Continuous directional forecast | `COST_SENSITIVE_NOT_PROMOTED` | [FUT-001 OOS](experiments/FUT-001-OOS.md) and the frozen [Bybit 2022 transfer](experiments/FUT-003-RESULT.md) each found a small positive 20bps arithmetic mean, negative compounding and negative 40bps mean across settlement bounds; month-block uncertainty spans zero. No validated directional engine. |
| 60-day time-series control | Selected directional comparator | `REPLICATION_FAILED_2022` | [FUT-002](experiments/FUT-002-RESULT.md) replicated on 2021–2022 with the exact frozen weights and costs. Positive 2021 gross information was concentrated early; 2022 gross was almost flat and net negative under every settlement bound. Do not tune the horizon or side to recover it. |
| Realized/EWMA volatility | Forecast normalization and separate risk size | `TESTED_WITH_WEAK_BASE` | FUT-001 fixed EWMA 0.94 and 5% annual floor. Its net portfolio failed cost robustness; this does not isolate the volatility component. A future ablation needs independent validation. |
| Point-in-time universe | Eligibility | `DATA_AUDIT` | 864 paired Binance USDT archive directories; 635 symbols with 2022–2025 rows and 517 with at least one eligible signal day. The [FUT-002 extension](experiments/FUT-002-DATA.md) adds 2020–2021 coverage and 247 checksum-verified daily repairs. The independent [Bybit source audit](INDEPENDENT_VENUE_DATA.md) found 15,509 causal 2022 eligible symbol-days across 106 names after checking observed bars, lagged turnover and funding; four potential cessation issues remain. Current metadata alone cannot define old membership. |
| Forced settlement | Exit/data handling | `BOUNDED_PROVISIONAL` | Seventy event/date candidates have checksum-verified minute trade/index archives and official notice leads. The [bounded OOS score](experiments/FUT-001-OOS.md) models forced exits and failed next-day orders; ten late-trade cases, exact second-level prices and account-tier fees still need audit. |
| Relative strength | Ranker | `UNTESTED_AS_INCREMENT` | Standalone quintiles failed in PR #31; after a viable base forecast, compare candidate selection with and without ranking. |
| Spot/perpetual basis | Distinct cross-sectional directional hypothesis | `GROSS_INFORMATION_FAILED_2022` | [FUT-004](experiments/FUT-004-RESULT.md) froze high-basis-long/low-basis-short daily quartiles before outcomes. The 2022 gross mean was negative across settlement stress; 2023 was approximately flat and the combined month-block interval included zero. This closes that exact one-day factor before cost modeling, without testing every basis or funding-carry mechanism. |
| Open interest | Positioning/participation context | `2022_PANEL_SOURCE_AUDITED_NO_SIGNAL` | The [Bybit OI source audit](POSITIONING_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) paginated all 190 existing 2022 price/funding candidates: 59,174 daily OI rows, 340 missing active price days, and 151 positive OI rows after GST's last price bar. Only 100 names have positive OI on every observed 2022 price day. OI sums both sides; a directional mechanism, publication lag, later-year coverage and fresh chronology are still required. |
| Funding and crowding | Derivatives context; separate lower-tail directional hypothesis | `FUT007_NO_SAMPLE_2023H2_FAILED_2024` | Historical funding is charged as a holding cost in earlier trials. The [product/source scout](FUNDING_DIRECTION_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) distinguishes two-leg carry from a single-leg signal and audited complete 2023–2025 ETH hourly prices and eight-hour funding. Frozen [FUT-007](experiments/FUT-007-RESULT.md) had only 13 events against a 15 minimum in 2023H2; 2024 had 52 events but negative event gross and costed net. Close that exact daily ETH lower-tail rule. Funding as context for a future viable base remains untested; carry is not standalone directional evidence. |
| COT | Context or possible regime feature; separate BTC-only directional hypothesis | `STANDALONE_GROSS_FAILED_2023` | The [CFTC audit](COT_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md) has 313 official 2020–2025 CME BTC records and explicit exceptional release lags. Frozen [FUT-005](experiments/FUT-005-RESULT.md) had a negative 2023 gross and 20bps net mean; 2024 positive arithmetic net compounded negatively. Close that exact paper-signed standalone rule. Incremental value as context for another viable base remains untested; COT is not a default mandatory gate. |
| Dollar-index macro forecast | Candidate daily BTC directional information | `FUT009_GROSS_INCREMENTAL_NET_FAILED_2024` | The [FUT-009 result](experiments/FUT-009-RESULT.md) scored a conservatively delayed sparse proxy after the source paper's window. On 209 active 2024 positions, price gross averaged −8.229 bp, 16.406 bp below the same-date always-long control; scheduled net was −6.403/−8.574 bp at 5/10 bp per side. Close the exact expanding-OLS perpetual rule; conditional 2025 was not scored. The paper's spot VAR, exact index and disputed fee accounting remain distinct. |
| Quarter-hour order flow | Candidate intraday directional information | `FUT006_GROSS_GATE_FAILED_POST_STUDY` | A [2026 primary study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09426) reports medium-horizon predictive content in six Binance perps through October 2024. The [source scout](ORDERFLOW_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) verified archive availability, and the [economic gate](ORDERFLOW_ECONOMIC_GATE.md) separated its sub-fee opening forecast from the post-bin regression. Frozen [FUT-006](experiments/FUT-006-RESULT.md) scored 122 delayed, nonoverlapping BTC/ETH intervals after the study: pooled gross −2.5332 bp and 5 bp-per-side net −7.4381 bp per interval; BTC/ETH diverged. Close that exact always-signed twelve-hour rule; other order-flow mechanisms and Hyperliquid transfer remain untested. |
| BTC-to-altcoin return lead-lag | Candidate intraday information diffusion | `FUT013_FROZEN_UNSCORED` | A [2024 spot study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165188924000551) reports that lagged BTC returns predict other coins over minutes. The [source scout](CROSS_CRYPTO_LEAD_LAG_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) verified nine official 2024/2025 BTC spot and ETH/SOL perp 1m ZIPs and 131,040 common minute clocks. [FUT-013](experiments/FUT-013.md) freezes a one-minute-delayed, ten-minute cost-aware sign transfer before reading conditioned returns; the spot paper is not a perp execution result. |
| Near-touch pressure versus capacity | Candidate order-book directional/entry state | `FUT008_GROSS_INCREMENTAL_NET_FAILED` | The frozen [FUT-008 result](experiments/FUT-008-RESULT.md) had 107/116 triggers on fixed 2023/2024 OKX days, but triggered-minus-all-short mid gross was −0.233/−0.318 bp and touch net at 5 bp/side was −10.286/−9.310 bp. Close the exact five-minute taker short. The [source study](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6693260) reports passive-buy toxicity and return associations, which this two-day pilot does not refute; entry avoidance could only be evaluated as a new incremental contract against an independently validated base. L2 sequence numbers, continuous-period behavior and Hyperliquid transfer remain unverified. |
| Hyperliquid loss-realizing close flow | Candidate venue-native directional information | `FUT010_FEE_FLOOR_FAILED_FUT011_INCREMENTAL_FAILED` | The [source audit](HYPERLIQUID_CLOSE_FLOW_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) verified wallet, realized-P&L, taker and liquidation fields in free 2025 mirror shards, while detecting internal gaps. Frozen [FUT-010](experiments/FUT-010-RESULT.md) scored 222 delayed BTC/ETH 30-minute returns: both dates below the 9 bp fee floor. Distinct frozen [FUT-011](experiments/FUT-011-RESULT.md) scored 118 delayed HYPE 120-minute returns: August below the fee floor and always-short, September above the fee floor but far below always-short. Close both exact all-wallet rules. The paper's point-in-time front-end wallet cohort remains untested; six 2025 order-ID API checks returned `unknownOid`. The distinct forced-HYPE trial is in the next row. |
| Hyperliquid market liquidation flow | Forced-close state, distinct from voluntary close flow | `FUT012_INCREMENTAL_FAILED` | The [return-free source audit](HYPERLIQUID_LIQUIDATION_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) established marker and backstop semantics; four complete 2025 days supplied 54 separated HYPE episodes. Frozen [FUT-012](experiments/FUT-012-RESULT.md) passed its sample gate and had +120.644 bp optimistic first-half gross, but second-half +9.931 bp lagged same-bin +19.791 bp always-short. Close its exact sign/timing rule; the August cascade-driven pooled result is not a validation. |
