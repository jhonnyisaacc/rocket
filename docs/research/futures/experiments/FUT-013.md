# FUT-013: delayed BTC-led altcoin minute return transfer

Status: `FROZEN_UNSCORED`, 2026-09-25. This contract is fixed before
any BTC-conditioned ETH/SOL future return is read. The prior is the
minute-level cross-crypto information-diffusion result in
[Guo et al. (2024)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165188924000551).
That paper studies spot coins and a broader statistical model. FUT-013
tests only a simple, costly-to-trade BTC spot sign transfer to perpetuals;
it cannot validate or refute the full paper.

## Causal rule and optimistic price proxy

At every UTC ten-minute boundary `t` whose full prediction and holding
window lies inside the same month, observe the **completed** BTC spot
minute `[t−1m,t)`. Set the signal to the sign of its `close/open − 1`:
positive predicts long ETH and long SOL, negative predicts short both,
zero predicts cash. This is a literal positive lead-lag transfer with
no fitted coefficient, threshold, asset choice, size filter or volatility
normalization. Also record the sign of each target's own completed
minute `[t−1m,t)` as a same-target lag control.

Wait one full minute after `t` before entry. The optimistic entry proxy
is the target perp's **open at `t+1m`**; exit is its open at `t+11m`,
exactly ten minutes later. Require positive base and quote volume in
the BTC predictor minute and in the target's own predictor, entry and
exit minute bars. An otherwise scheduled event failing these checks,
having a zero BTC return, or lacking either price proxy is cash with
its reason counted. Source gaps are a source failure. No trading at the
signal minute close and no use of its later target price. Ten-minute
boundary spacing makes ten-minute holds nonoverlapping per target;
ETH and SOL positions can be concurrent.

For each active target event, gross bp is
`10,000 × BTC_signal × (exit_open / entry_open − 1)`.
Score same-event always-long, always-short and own-prior-minute-sign
gross controls on the **same entry and exit prices**. Report mean and
median gross, `gross − 9 bp` as the intended Hyperliquid base taker
round-trip fee floor, and `gross − 20 bp` as stress. These are an
optimistic transfer screen: Binance open prices are not executable
Hyperliquid fills, and spread, impact, depth, latency and funding are
not included. Any pass requires those costs and venue transfer before
a net or production claim.
