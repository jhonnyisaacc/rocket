# FUT-006: quarter-hour opening imbalance, bounded post-study falsifier

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_ORDERFLOW_CONDITIONED_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24.
This is one new BTC/ETH directional trial, motivated by the medium-horizon
finding in [Kim and Hansen (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09426v2),
Section 6. The [economic gate](../ORDERFLOW_ECONOMIC_GATE.md) separates it
from that paper's separate, sub-fee first-ten-second *return forecast*.

## Question and decision rule

Does the **sign of buyer-minus-seller aggregate-trade volume during the
first ten seconds of a quarter hour** predict the subsequent twelve-hour
BTC/ETH perpetual return after a ten-second processing delay, and pay for
actual position changes, funding and a taker-cost floor?

Use **2024-11-01 through 2024-12-31 UTC** as a bounded post-paper falsifier:
122 nonoverlapping twelve-hour entry opportunities per contract. This period
begins after the paper's October 2024 sample. Other Rocket experiments have
inspected unconditional late-2024 BTC/ETH prices, so it is post-paper but not
a pristine project-wide holdout. No order-flow-conditioned result from this
window has been inspected. Score the pooled equal-notional BTC/ETH portfolio
at every scheduled interval, including losing and unchanged-target intervals.
The cheap gate requires **positive pooled gross mean** and **positive pooled
net mean at both 5 and 10 bp per executed taker side** across the complete
window. If either fails, close this exact always-signed, twelve-hour rule;
report November/December, BTC/ETH, sign, turnover, funding and concentration
even on failure. Do not tune the sign, selected hours, cost, coin, bin, delay
or horizon from these results. A pass only authorizes a frozen 2025
replication, then venue transfer and later holdout work; it is not promotion.

## Causal contract

- Binance USD-M public daily BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT `aggTrades` ZIPs, with their
  official `.CHECKSUM` files, are the sole signal and price tape. Audit their
  schema, ZIP hash, UTC date, timestamp and aggregate-ID ordering, adjacent
  day ID continuity, and signal/entry/exit window presence before scoring.
  The [source scout](../ORDERFLOW_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) already verified the two
  January 1, 2025 ZIPs, used here only for the final 2024 exit price.
- At UTC `00:15:00` and `12:15:00` on each study day, compute
  `OI = sum(quantity * (+1 if is_buyer_maker=false else -1)) / sum(quantity)`
  from aggregate trades timestamped in `[T,T+10 seconds)`. The target is
  `sign(OI)` BTC or ETH notional, zero only when OI is exactly zero. A missing
  or empty signal bin is an unresolved source condition, never a guessed
  zero. No threshold, magnitude weight, price filter or fitted parameter.
- The decision is available at `T+10s`. Allow ten further seconds for
  processing and order submission. Use the first observed aggregate-trade
  price in `[T+20s,T+30s)` as an **optimistic execution proxy**; the signal
  does not use trades after `T+10s`. Mark each interval from that entry
  proxy to the same clock's proxy twelve hours later. A missing proxy or
  discontinuity while exposed is unresolved, not silently dropped.
  Neither trade-price proxy proves a taker fill or spread.
- Each contract has a continuing ±1/0 target across the 122 adjacent
  intervals; no overlapping positions, leverage above 1x, or interim stop.
  Portfolio gross return per interval is the arithmetic mean of the two
  signed contract price returns. Start flat and charge final flattening to
  the last interval. Charge transaction cost at each scheduled rebalance as
  `side_bps * abs(new_target - old_target) / 10_000` per contract, including
  the initial open, reversals and final exit; portfolio costs are averaged
  across the two contracts. Primary `side_bps=5` matches the paper's
  standard Binance taker fee scale (10 bp flat round trip). Stress
  `side_bps=10` allows another 5 bp each way for unobserved spread/impact.
  [Current Hyperliquid tier-zero taker fees](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees)
  are 4.5 bp per side; this is context, not an assumed historical fill.
- Funding is charged as `target * sum(actual Binance funding rates)` for
  funding slots in `(entry_time, exit_time]`, using the checksum-verified
  normalized FUT-002 Binance funding database. Audit all expected slots
  while exposed. Monthly results are grouped by entry timestamp, with the
  final flattening cost in December. Cash earns zero. Use log-price return
  only as a secondary check; primary portfolio accounting uses simple
  `exit_price/entry_price - 1` per interval.

## Interpretation and controls

Report cash and an always-long BTC/ETH comparator at the same scheduled
entry/exit proxies, funding and turnover rules. Also report each asset's
signed gross information and net return separately, November and December
separately, long/short counts, daily-block bootstrap interval for pooled
mean, total and funding/transaction cost contributions, compounded return,
and the share of gross P&L in the best five UTC days. Daily blocks preserve
within-day contract dependence; the bootstrap is diagnostic at this cheap
gate and cannot rescue a nonpositive frozen point estimate. Use seed
`20260924`, 10,000 resamples.

The paper's full-sample regression slope is neither a direct strategy return
nor a zero-delay fill. This trial is a narrower, fixed nonoverlapping use of
its mechanism; failure closes only this contract. A positive result is still
optimistic because trade prices are proxies and the source venue differs
from intended Hyperliquid execution. The 2025 order-flow-conditioned results
and 2026 final holdout remain unread before this frozen gate. Execution stays
disabled.
