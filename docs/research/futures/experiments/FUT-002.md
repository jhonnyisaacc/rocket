# FUT-002: historical replication of the frozen 60-day control

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_2021_2022_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24. This successor question is motivated by the 60-day control's stronger 2025 point estimate in [FUT-001](FUT-001-OOS.md), so it counts as a new trial. No 2021 or 2022 return for this control or the multi-horizon comparator has been inspected. The 2026 final holdout remains untouched.

## Question and falsifier

Does the exact 60-day normalized trend control retain positive net portfolio expectancy in **both** previously unscored 2021 and 2022 calendar years on a broad point-in-time Binance USD-M USDT perpetual tape, after funding and 20/40bps round-trip costs? The mechanism is directional persistence over approximately three months without averaging the 20- and 120-day forecasts. Replication fails if the 60-day portfolio's mean daily net return is nonpositive in either year at 20bps, or if the two-year combined mean is nonpositive at 40bps. Passing these simple checks earns further independent venue/cost work; it does not promote the strategy because 2023–2024 discovery was negative and this trial was selected after seeing 2025.

## Frozen implementation

- Reuse the exact FUT-001 60-day control forecast, EWMA volatility estimator, 5% annual floor, 121 completed daily-bar history, lagged 30-day USD 5 million quote-volume floor, historical funding coverage, point-in-time eligibility, fixed denominator and 5% individual weight cap. Do not change frequency, thresholds, side, sizing, or costs for this test. Cash and the original 20/60/120-day portfolio are comparators; do not choose the better comparator after the run.
- Acquire all coverage-inventory USD-M USDT names with paired price and funding files in 2020–2021, with the previously acquired 2022 files. Verify every ZIP against the source `.CHECKSUM`, normalize into a **new versioned database**, and preserve the original 2022–2025 FUT-001 database and report hashes. 2020 is warmup only; score 2021 and 2022 by next daily open, excluding a last entry whose exit would cross the year boundary. Do not use a current exchange list or a future-survival filter.
- Reuse the FUT-001 20bps and 40bps round-trip cost model plus actual timestamped funding. On a contract cessation, document the notice cutoff and affected symbol, check minute index/trade archives and apply the same predeclared adverse/middle/favorable cutoff, price and funding bounds and 5% price stress. A next-day order without a valid open remains cash; a held position cannot disappear. Report unresolved exposed outcomes and stop performance interpretation if they cannot be defensibly bounded.
- Report year-specific and combined mean daily net, compounded return, Sharpe, drawdown, turnover, funding, gross predictive statistic, sides, asset concentration, and calendar-month bootstrap uncertainty. Do not claim statistical independence of overlapping symbol-days. No plateau search, best side/coin slice, or 2026 data access.

This is a retrospective replication of a selected control, not a fresh prospective validation. A later candidate would still need historical stability, account-tier venue costs, Hyperliquid overlap and a separate frozen forward shadow before an `ENTER_*` contract is considered.
