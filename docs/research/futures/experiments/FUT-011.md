## Question and source boundary

Does the direction opposite **voluntary, loss-realizing HYPE taker closes**
predict a price move over 120 minutes after observation and entry delay that
exceeds Hyperliquid's 9 bp base taker round-trip fee floor and both same-bin
directional controls on two separated 2025 windows? The presentation shows
HYPE close-loser mid-price markouts continuing to reverse between 30 and
120 minutes, and similar curves across wallet groups after conditioning on
close-loser state. Its chart is an event study, not a delayed executable
return. We use all wallets because the free fill mirror lacks order `tif`;
six historical fill wallet/`oid` probes returned `unknownOid` from today's
order-status API. This trial does not claim to reproduce the front-end
wallet cohort or trade forced liquidations.

## Causal rule and optimistic price proxy

Schedule a decision at every UTC five-minute boundary `t` for which the
complete entry and exit proxy windows remain inside the same fixed segment.
Observe fills by **block time** in `[t,t+5m)` only if their local receipt is
no later than `t+6m`. A qualifying fill has `crossed=true`, `dir` exactly
`Close Long` or `Close Short`, negative `closedPnl`, and no `liquidation`
object. Use its `px * sz` USD notional. Set
`S = sum(sell-close notional) - sum(buy-close notional)`, where a close-long
sell must be `side=A` and a close-short buy must be `side=B`. Predict long
for `S>0`, short for `S<0`, cash for zero. Reject inconsistent side/close
directions as a source error. Do not threshold size, select a wallet group,
or inspect future prices before calculating `S`.

Decision time is `t+6m`: one minute after the observation bin. Use the
size-weighted VWAP of unique market trades in `[t+6m,t+6m+10s)` as an
optimistic entry price proxy. Exit at the same proxy in
`[t+126m,t+126m+10s)`, exactly 120 minutes after entry. A unique trade is
its one taker fill and `(block_number, coin, tid)`. Missing either proxy
means cash on that scheduled opportunity, reported separately. The 7-hour
segment permits starts from 03:00 through 07:50 inclusive at five-minute
steps: at most 59 opportunities per date and 118 total. Positions overlap;
these are equal-notional event returns, not independent trades or a
self-financing portfolio.

For each active opportunity, gross price return in bp is
`10_000 * direction * (exit_px / entry_px - 1)`. Report same-bin always-long
and always-short gross controls; `gross - 9 bp` for the base taker fee floor;
and `gross - 20 bp` for a 10 bp-per-side stress. Trade VWAP omits entry/exit
spread, impact, queue, and latency, so it is optimistic. Funding is omitted
and must be added before any net or execution claim. A pass only authorizes
an official-source cross-check, wider chronological replication, order-book
and funding costs, and a newly frozen validation contract. No side, sign,
clock, wallet, threshold, horizon, date, or subwindow salvage is allowed
after this result.

signed-notional sign balance, and each date's mean gross, median gross,
