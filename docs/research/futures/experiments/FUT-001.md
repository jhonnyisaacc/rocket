Changes to the rule after a scored run require a successor ID; corrections to data parsing must preserve the original run and document the bug. ## Question and cheapest falsifier

Does a continuous, volatility-normalized, multi-horizon time-series trend forecast produce positive chronological out-of-sample net return on a broad point-in-time Binance USD-M USDT perpetual universe? The mechanism is directional persistence over roughly one, three and six calendar months. The null controls are cash (zero) and a single 60-day normalized trend with the same universe, risk construction and costs. A nonpositive 2025 mean daily portfolio return at 20bps round trip plus funding rejects this exact base strategy. Positive at 20bps but nonpositive at 40bps is cost-sensitive, not promoted. Positive means only further research, never live acceptance.

## Data and point-in-time universe

- Source: official monthly USD-M `klines/<symbol>/1d/` and `fundingRate/<symbol>/` ZIP archives, each verified against its `.CHECKSUM`, with raw file hashes and acquisition times retained. Consider every raw symbol ending `USDT` in the **intersection** of price and funding archive directories, including symbols later delisted. No present-day exchange-info or market-cap list. The directory intersection generates candidates only; it never grants eligibility at a past timestamp.
- Use 2022-01-01 through 2025-12-31 UTC archives. 2022 is initial warmup; 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31 is discovery and mechanics check; **2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31 is the single cheap-gate OOS evaluation**. All of 2026 is an untouched final holdout. If the raw dataset cannot support 2025 as declared, stop and record a data failure rather than silently move the window.
- Partition by the **entry open**. Score only a daily holding interval whose next open is inside the same declared partition; the last scored OOS entry is 2025-12-30 for a 2025-12-31 exit. No 2026 observation is used even as an exit price. Report any boundary positions as unscored/censored rather than moving them into the adjacent partition's mean.
- At completed day `t` (23:59:59.999 UTC), a symbol is eligible only if it has 121 consecutive valid completed daily bars ending at `t`, 30-day mean **observed quote volume** at least USD 5 million, and timestamped funding coverage in the preceding 30 days. Use the archive's quote asset volume column, not base volume times a later price. A zero-volume or flat OHLC day invalidates that day's eligibility; flag it for audit rather than inferring an active contract. No bar or funding observation after `t` influences eligibility. A symbol enters when it first satisfies the rule and exits when it ceases; no future survival criterion.
- Validate timestamps, duplicates, contiguous days, positive OHLC, `low <= open/close <= high`, nonnegative volume, and exact UTC alignment. Historical funding intervals may change: use each record's `funding_interval_hours` and actual `calc_time`; require a complete expected schedule in the 30-day lookback. Duplicate or missing settlements are not zero-funded. Record exclusion counts by symbol/day and missing archive months. A missing source file is a data gap, not a zero-volume month.
- A missing next open or funding settlement **on an exposed day** produces an unresolved outcome. Do not omit that day or set funding to zero. Report its gross exposure, date, symbol, and sensitivity; the primary evaluation cannot pass while an unresolved exposure remains. This deliberately prevents delisting or provider gaps from disappearing from the PnL. Attempt to find documented settlement data or a defensible conservative bound before scoring.

## Forecast and portfolio, fixed before PnL

- At the close of completed day `t`, calculate daily log returns and EWMA daily volatility with decay 0.94 over the latest 120 returns. Initialize using the first 30 returns; no future return in the recursion. Floor annualized volatility at 5% and divide by `sqrt(365)` for a daily sigma. This simple estimator is the baseline; no GARCH.
- Horizons are **20, 60, 120 completed days**. For each `h`, `z_h = log(close_t / close_(t-h)) / (sigma_t * sqrt(h))`; `f_h = clip(z_h, -2, 2) / 2`. Continuous forecast `f_t = (f_20 + f_60 + f_120)/3`, bounded in [-1, 1]. Forecast sign is direction; forecast magnitude is strength. The single-horizon control uses `f_60` alone. These values represent one, three, six approximate months and are not fitted to a return table.
- Risk size is separate: for eligible symbol `i`, `raw_i = f_i / annualized_sigma_i`, denominator `D = sum_j (1 / annualized_sigma_j)` over all eligible symbols at `t`; desired weight at the next open is `clip(raw_i / D, -0.05, 0.05)`. Ineligible names receive zero. Thus gross notional is at most 1x and an individual name at most 5%; weak forecasts do not force full deployment. No correlation filter, one-position throttle, stop, target, or leverage optimization in this signal test.
- Compute the desired weights at `t` close, fill at the **next** daily open `d=t+1`, and hold to the following daily open. A missing exact open is unresolved, never substituted with a close. Daily gross portfolio return is `sum_i w_(i,d) * (open_(i,d+1)/open_(i,d) - 1)`. Funding drag is `sum_i w_(i,d) * sum(funding rates with actual settlement time in (open_d, open_(d+1)])`; a positive rate costs a long. This uses entry notional as the funding approximation and must be stress-tested because actual mark notional moves.
- Charge `round_trip_bps/2` per unit of absolute notional change at each open, including entry and exit. Primary cost scenarios: **20bps and 40bps round trip**, each plus funding. Compute turnover against the prior target weight (a fixed-notional approximation, disclosed separately from an account simulator). Do not count only winning fills or quietly zero missing costs. Cash earns zero.

## Validation protocol and required report

1. Complete and hash the normalized data acceptance report before scoring: symbols, monthly-file coverage, bar/funding gaps, matched settlements, date range, and unresolved exposure policy. Keep raw ZIP hashes. A parsed file may be corrected for a demonstrated defect before outcomes; freeze the accepted dataset version.
2. Run exactly the primary forecast, cash, and 60-day control on 2023–2024 discovery and 2025 OOS. Report daily net mean, compounded return, annualized volatility/Sharpe, drawdown, turnover, gross return, funding, and transaction drag at both cost levels. Separately report the equal-weight mean of `forecast_i,t * next_open_return_i` over eligible symbol-days; this is the continuous forecast's gross predictive statistic, not portfolio PnL or a costed strategy. Cash has zero return by definition.
3. Report per-year, quarter, long/short contribution, symbol concentration, effective breadth, unique active names, coverage/exclusion counts, and dates of unresolved outcomes. Use monthly or block bootstrap uncertainty because daily and cross-sectional observations are dependent; no naive independent-event t-test. The 2025 OOS result is the cheap gate, not an untouched final holdout.
4. If the primary passes, run predeclared plateau checks at 15/45/90 and 30/90/180 day horizons with the same remaining rules; count each as a separate robustness trial, never choose the best. Escalate to anchored walk-forward windows ending in 2023, 2024, 2025, multiple-trial-aware uncertainty, then Hyperliquid overlap. Keep 2026 untouched until a promotion candidate is frozen. If the primary fails, record the failure and reassess the frontier; do not rescue by choosing a plateau variant.
