# FUT-003: Bybit venue transfer of the frozen trend baseline

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_BYBIT_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24. No Bybit trend forecast-times-return, portfolio return, Sharpe, or asset/side slice has been inspected. This is another trial of the **unchanged FUT-001 multi-horizon rule** on a different venue, not a new strategy and not an independent market regime: Binance 2022 was already examined in FUT-002. Its purpose is to learn whether the weak gross information and costs of the old baseline transfer across venues. Neither a positive nor negative Bybit score can promote a different mechanism.

## Question and falsifier

On a broad point-in-time Bybit USDT-perpetual 2022 tape, does the exact FUT-001 multi-horizon forecast have a positive equal-weight gross predictive statistic and positive mean daily portfolio net after both 20bps and 40bps round-trip costs plus timestamped funding? A nonpositive gross statistic rejects the directional information on this venue. A positive gross statistic with nonpositive 20bps or 40bps mean marks the old baseline cost-sensitive or failed; do not tune weights, horizon, side or symbol subset. Cash and the exact 60-day FUT-001 control are fixed comparators.

## Frozen construction and data

- Reuse FUT-001's 20/60/120-day forecast, EWMA 0.94 volatility with 5% annual floor, 121 consecutive completed bars, lagged 30-day USDT 5 million turnover floor, 30-day prior funding check, capped fixed-denominator weights, next-open fill and following-open exit, and rejected unfilled orders. The 60-day control differs only in forecast horizon. Use 2020–2021 only as available warmup and score 2022 entry opens through December 30; never use a 2023 exit. Charge 20bps and 40bps round trip plus actual Bybit funding as historical sensitivity, without claiming these are account-tier executable costs.
- Candidate symbols are all 190 `Trading` or `Closed` USDT linear perpetuals with reported launch before 2023 in the hashed current Bybit catalog. The catalog generates candidates only. Day-specific eligibility comes solely from completed observed bars, lagged turnover and funding; an instrument remains eligible through a valid prior day even if it later delists. The 2022 return-free preflight found 15,509 eligible symbol-days in 106 names and four possible closure-related issues. Do not silently omit a held position whose next open disappears.
- For LUNA May 12, use the [Bybit API announcement's reported 0.024 USDT at 10:03 UTC](https://t.me/s/Bybit_API_Announcements?before=94) as the middle forced-settlement price, with a separate ±5% price sensitivity. The minute index at 10:03 matches 0.024; the preceding 30-minute average is **not** substituted because it conflicts with the announced special settlement. For FTT November 13 12:00 and SRM November 15 03:00, use the union of complete 30- and 60-minute index-OHLC mean envelopes around the metadata delivery cutoff, then extend by ±5% price stress. These are provisional bounds until historical venue rules/exact prices are obtained. Include funding through each cutoff and boundary sensitivity; an attempted next-day order without an open remains cash. If any held outcome cannot be represented defensibly, mark the score incomplete rather than claim a pass.
- Report gross forecast statistic, annual and quarterly net/compounded/Sharpe/drawdown, gross exposure, sides, asset concentration, turnover, funding, forced settlements, rejected orders and month-block uncertainty. Outcome interpretation must discuss the current-catalog completeness limit, historical Bybit fee uncertainty, one malformed BCH first bar and the three settlement-price cases.
