**new sparse-proxy venue-transfer trial**, not a replication of the

## Mechanism and cheap falsifier

The [daily macro study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/for.70077)
reports economically large spot-BTC gross returns from a dollar-index
forecast in a sample ending February 2024. A change in the dollar may
precede a slower repricing of BTC risk demand. The effect may disappear
after the original period, after a causal source delay, on a perpetual,
or after trading costs. Fit one expanding, single-predictor linear forecast
using **only previously resolved** BTC outcomes and the dollar-index proxy;
trade its sign for one day. The slope is estimated from past data, so no
paper-sign assumption, magnitude threshold or chosen market regime enters.

Score the **2024-03-01 through 2024-12-29 New York dollar-session-date
window first** (post-study); the first eligible entry is 2024-03-02 04:00
UTC, and all exits precede 2025. The frozen pass requires:

1. At least **150 active positions** with no unresolved active price or
   funding exposure.
2. Positive mean active-position BTC price gross return and positive mean
   active-position gross **minus always-long gross on those same dates**.
3. Positive mean **scheduled-calendar-day net** at both **5 and 10 bp per
   executed side**, including actual eight-hour funding and all target
   changes, initial entry and terminal flattening.

If any gate fails, close this exact rule. Do not change the dollar lag,
source holiday handling, estimation sample, side, hold, regression,
threshold, fill clock or cost after the result. If all pass, score the
unchanged **2025-01-01 through 2025-12-29 dollar-session-date window**
under the same gates as chronological replication. That result can justify
intended-venue transfer and forward shadow, not a live trade. The 2026
final holdout remains untouched. A pass in one market year is not proof of
stability.

## Exact point-in-time rule

- Source: the acquired Yahoo `DX-Y.NYB` `INDEX` proxy, not the Federal
  Reserve broad dollar index or USDX futures settlement. Use the ignored
  [as-of manifest](../MACRO_DOLLAR_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) unchanged. For a New
  York source session date `D`, its close first becomes eligible at
  **03:00 UTC on calendar date `D+1`**, later than the documented ICE
  weekday index publication endpoint. A missing Yahoo source day is
  **cash**, never a carried-forward new signal. Source daily timestamps
  are session labels, not release times. This sparse rule does not claim
  complete ICE calendar coverage or exact paper data.
- Let `C_D` and `C_prev` be the current and prior **valid** proxy closes.
  If either is absent or their New York session dates are over **four
  calendar days** apart, target cash. Otherwise `x_D = ln(C_D/C_prev)`.
  No magnitude screen, winsorization, post hoc sign inversion or holiday
  fill. Use only one source row per day.
- On every UTC calendar date from **2024-03-02 through 2024-12-30** in
  discovery, set target at **04:00 UTC** from the immediately preceding
  calendar date `D` only if that date has an eligible source close.
  The one-hour gap after the 03:00 as-of time is processing latency. Fill
  optimistically at the official BTCUSDT 1h candle open at 04:00 UTC,
  and mark the 24-hour return at the following day's 04:00 UTC candle
  open. Every scheduled interval is nonoverlapping. The final position is
  flattened at its exit clock. The analogous 2025 entry dates are
  **2025-01-02 through 2025-12-30**, conditional on the 2024 pass.
- Training pairs start at source date **2022-01-03**. Pair `j` uses its
  eligible `x_j` and the log BTCUSDT 04:00-to-next-04:00 price return.
  At a decision `T` of 03:00 UTC, admit a training pair **only if its
  exit clock is strictly before `T`**; thus the immediately preceding
  daily target cannot leak into the forecast. Refit an expanding OLS
  `y_j = alpha + beta*x_j` at each eligible decision with at least **200
  complete past pairs**. If fewer, cash. A zero predictor variance or
  nonfinite fit is unresolved, never substituted with an arbitrary side.
  Forecast `alpha + beta*x_D`; target +1 long if positive, −1 short if
  negative, and 0 cash if exactly zero. The model uses no same-day future
  BTC return, funding, threshold, fitted percentile or discretionary
  model selection.
- Trading price return is `target*(exit_open/entry_open - 1)` for each
  24-hour interval; cash earns zero. Funding drag is
  `target * sum(rate)` using actual BTCUSDT settlement timestamps in
  `(entry, exit]`; a positive rate costs a long. At every 04:00 UTC
  boundary charge `side_bps/10000 * abs(new_target-old_target)`, with
  `old_target=0` before the first interval, and charge terminal flattening
  at the last exit. This includes two sides on a direct reversal and no
  cost for maintaining the same target. Primary 5 bp/side and stress
  10 bp/side are each above or near the intended venue's base taker rate;
  extra spread/impact and funding-basis errors remain risks, not gains.
