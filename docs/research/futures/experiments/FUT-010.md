# FUT-010: delayed loss-close reversal, fixed 2025 gross pilot

Status: `FROZEN_UNSCORED`, 2026-09-24. Freeze this contract and the source
window before reading any loss-close-conditioned future price return. This
is a cheap directional falsifier, not a complete Hyperliquid execution
backtest or a replication of the source paper's front-end wallet cohort.

## Question and prior

Can the direction opposite **all-wallet, voluntary, loss-realizing taker
closes** predict a delayed BTC/ETH perpetual price move large enough to
clear Hyperliquid's **9 bp base taker round-trip fee floor**? The
[source study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7270041)
reports eventual reversal after its classified front-end traders' loss
closes, but gives no executable effect size in the public abstract. Our
all-wallet rule is a distinct, weaker implementation. It does not test
forced liquidations. The [source audit](../HYPERLIQUID_CLOSE_FLOW_SOURCE_SCOUT.md)
verified field presence and event counts without calculating outcomes.

## Causal rule and optimistic price proxy

For each coin independently, make scheduled decisions at UTC five-minute
boundaries `t` inside each segment. Observe fills whose **block time** is
in `[t,t+5m)` and whose `local_time` is no later than `t+6m`. A qualifying
fill has `crossed=true`, `dir` exactly `Close Long` or `Close Short`,
`closedPnl < 0`, and no `liquidation` object. Weight it by `px * sz` USD.
Define `S = sum(sell-close notional) - sum(buy-close notional)`; sell closes
are `side=A` and buy closes `side=B`. `S>0` predicts **long**, `S<0`
predicts **short**, and `S=0` means cash. Unknown side/direction
combinations are a source error, not silently assigned a sign. This is the
loss-close reversal sign fixed before any return inspection.

The intended decision time is `t+6m`, one minute after the observation bin
closes. Use the VWAP of unique market trades in `[t+6m,t+6m+10s)` as an
**optimistic entry price proxy** and the same proxy in
`[t+36m,t+36m+10s)` as exit. A trade is uniquely represented by its one
`crossed=true` fill and `(block_number, coin, tid)`. These observed trade
prices do not establish Rocket's bid/ask, queue, impact, or latency. If
either proxy is missing, score the scheduled opportunity as cash and report
it. Only schedule `t` when the complete entry and exit windows remain inside
the same verified segment. This permits starts 14:00–17:20 on July 28,
00:00–01:20 and 10:00–14:20 on October 10, inclusive every five minutes;
at most 222 coin-decisions. Several thirty-minute positions overlap; this
pilot scores equal-notional **event returns**, not a self-financing
portfolio or independent trades.

For an active event, price gross in bp is
`10_000 * direction * (exit_px / entry_px - 1)`, with long `+1` and short
`-1`. Report the same eligible-bin always-long and always-short gross
controls. Report `gross - 9 bp` as the base taker-fee floor proxy and
`gross - 20 bp` as a stricter 10 bp-per-side proxy. Spread, impact and
funding are omitted optimistically; a pass would trigger their measurement
before a net claim. No post-result sign inversion, side, coin, date,
wallet, threshold, horizon, or clock selection is allowed under FUT-010.

## Cheap gate and interpretation

First report source coverage, active signal count, cash reasons, and
signed-notional sign balance by coin and date. Require at least 50 active
events on **each** fixed date and 150 in total. If the count fails, status
is `NO_SAMPLE`; do not infer performance. If it passes, require on **each**
date both (a) mean active price gross **greater than 9 bp** and (b) mean
active price gross **greater than both** same-date, same-bin always-long
and always-short controls. Failure of either is `GROSS_OR_FEE_FLOOR_FAILED`
for this exact rule. Report pooled mean and median, coin/date breakdown,
10 bp-per-side stress, and an hour-block interval as diagnostics, with no
subgroup salvage. A pass only authorizes an official-archive cross-check,
broader chronological replication, actual bid/ask and funding costs, and a
new frozen contract. The 2026 final holdout and production scan remain
untouched.
