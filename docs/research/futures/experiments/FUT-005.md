# FUT-005: Published noncommercial BTC positioning change

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_COT_CONDITIONED_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24. This is one
new directional trial. Its sign and normalization are motivated by
[Dunbar and Owusu-Amoako (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2023.100812),
which reported a positive weekly return coefficient for changes in CME BTC
noncommercial net-short positioning over its 2018–September 2022 sample.
That paper is an external discovery prior, not a Rocket validation or a
promise that a costed, publication-lagged perp strategy works. The paper's
"retail" interpretation of noncommercial accounts is not assumed.

## Mechanism, question and falsifier

The candidate mechanism is information in *changes* of reportable speculative
positioning across the CME BTC futures market that persists after its public
release and transfers to the Binance BTC perpetual. This is distinct from
perpetual price trend and spot/perpetual basis ranking. It is BTC-only by
construction, with no current-universe projection or selected altcoin.

Question: Does the **signed change in normalized noncommercial net-short
positioning** predict subsequent BTCUSDT perpetual returns after the CFTC
release delay, and survive funding and turnover? On each held day the gross
informational statistic is position sign times next-open BTC return. The
cheap gate is positive calendar-year mean daily gross and positive
calendar-year mean daily **20bps round-trip plus funding net** in *both*
2023 and 2024. Failure of either year closes this exact always-signed
contract. Report combined calendar-month bootstrap uncertainty even if the
point signs pass. Score 2025 at 20bps and 40bps only if the 2023/2024 gate
passes. Never inspect a COT-conditioned 2026 result under this trial.

The study's 2018–2022 period already informs the sign; 2020–2022 rows supply
source history and warmup, not fresh evidence. Other Rocket trials have
inspected unconditioned BTC outcomes in 2023–2025, so 2023–2025 is a
chronological COT-specific replication, not a pristine project-wide holdout.
The 2026 final holdout and a frozen Hyperliquid forward shadow remain later
promotion gates.

## Exact causal rule

- Source is the six official legacy **futures-only** annual CFTC ZIPs and
  code `133741`, audited [here](../COT_SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md). Define
  `b_t = (noncommercial_short_t - noncommercial_long_t) /
  (noncommercial_short_t + noncommercial_long_t)` for as-of week `t`.
  Define `delta_t = b_t - b_(t-1)` using the preceding as-of report.
  `sign(delta_t)` is `+1` long, `-1` short, or zero cash. There is no fitted
  coefficient, percentile, deadband, absolute level test, or volatility
  scaling. A sign reversal is a new trial, not a FUT-005 rescue.
- Each report is first usable on the `available_utc_date` frozen by
  `cftc_cot_source_audit.py`: ordinary as-of plus ten calendar days, with
  2023/2025 publication exceptions delayed further. Use only the latest
  actually available as-of row as of each UTC entry open. Hold the existing
  signal through days without a new report. If its as-of date is more than
  21 calendar days old at entry, flatten to cash; a late report already
  older than 21 days cannot initiate a position. A new report always uses
  the preceding **as-of** record for its delta, even if that older report
  arrived late or was skipped for trading. No raw Tuesday positions enter
  the portfolio before publication.
- Binance BTCUSDT USD-M 1d perp bars are the research price tape; every
  2020–2025 day is observed and tradable in the repaired database. At an
  eligible UTC daily open set target weight to the signal sign, with maximum
  absolute notional 1x. Hold open-to-next-open; mark gross as
  `weight * (next_open/open - 1)`. Annual daily means include cash days at
  zero return and zero cost. Carry the previous executed weight across days.
  An unavailable price or funding interval while exposed is unresolved,
  not silently excluded. Partition by entry open. For 2023/2024, close the
  final position at the next January 1 open, but do not move that interval
  into the next year's mean. For conditional 2025, the last scored entry is
  December 30 with a December 31 exit, so no 2026 bar is read. Charge any
  terminal exit turnover to that partition's last scored day.
- Funding drag is signed weight times the sum of actual Binance funding
  rates in `(entry_open, exit_open]`. Charge `round_trip_bps/2` per unit of
  absolute weight change at each open, including opening, reversal and
  eventual exit; primary 20bps and stress 40bps. These are research cost
  assumptions, not actual account fills. Cash earns zero. Daily open is an
  optimistic executable proxy; a positive trial requires later delayed-fill
  work.

## Controls and interpretation

Report cash; always-long BTC on every day when a nonzero, nonstale COT signal
exists, with the same funding and costs; and a price-only
`sign(log(close_(d-1)/close_(d-8)))` control traded at the same daily opens
and restricted to those same COT-active days. The seven-day
control is a predeclared ablation of incremental positioning information,
not an alternative selected successor. Report year, month, long/short,
turnover, gross, funding and transaction contributions, compounded return,
drawdown, and the effect of 2023/2025 publication disruptions. Use a fixed
10,000-resample calendar-month bootstrap with seed `20260924`; no naive
independent-day significance claim.

A cheap-gate pass permits the conditional 2025 check, not strategy promotion.
A 2025 survivor needs 40bps net robustness, incremental evidence over the
price-only control, direct Hyperliquid overlap, costed delayed fills,
venue-specific execution and a prospective frozen shadow before any Rocket
`ENTER_*` integration. If the gross gate fails, stop without reversing sign,
selecting a COT subgroup, shortening publication lag or adding a threshold.
Execution remains disabled.
