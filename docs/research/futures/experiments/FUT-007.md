# FUT-007: lower-tail funding as an ETH directional transfer

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_ETH_FUNDING_CONDITIONED_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24.
The independent prior is [Memon et al. (2026)](https://www.aijmr.com/papers/2026/4/1493.pdf),
which finds positive subsequent BTC *spot* returns after the lower historical
decile of BTC perpetual funding. The [source/product-fit scout](../FUNDING_DIRECTION_SOURCE_SCOUT.md)
distinguishes this directional question from a two-leg carry hedge. Its BTC
sample extends through August 2026; this is **ETH asset transfer**, not a
chronologically post-paper result or a direct Hyperliquid test.

## Mechanism, schedule and falsifier

Unusually low funding can mark crowded short positioning or a perp discount
that is followed by a one-day rebound. It can also merely reflect a prior
price fall, and negative funding credits to a long are not a guarantee that
the price will rise. Test a **long-only ETH perpetual target** after a lower
funding percentile; cash otherwise. Never short upper-tail funding under
this trial, because the source paper found no symmetric longer-horizon
effect. Its authors do not claim a trading strategy.

Warm up on ETH funding from **2023-01-01 through 2023-06-30**. Score the fixed
daily 01:00 UTC schedule in **2023-07-01–2023-12-31** and the full **2024**
year as two separate partitions, with initial and terminal flattening in
each. If either partition has fewer than **15 triggered days**, label the
cheap gate `NO_SAMPLE` rather than claim success. Otherwise pass only if
**each partition** has positive triggered-interval gross mean, positive
triggered-versus-all-day gross difference, and positive scheduled-day net
mean at **5 bp per executed side**, and their combined scheduled-day net
mean is positive at **10 bp per side**. Report uncertainty and concentration
regardless of point-estimate sign. A pass permits the same fixed rule on
conditional **2025** data; 2026 is reserved for a later final holdout.
This demanding gate recognizes that a positive long return during a bull
year is not enough to establish funding-specific information. Failure
closes this exact daily lower-tail ETH contract, without threshold, side,
hour, asset or window-length rescue.

## Exact causal construction

- Source: the official, checksum-verified 2023–2025 Binance ETHUSDT 1h
  USD-M candle ZIPs and the normalized, checksum-repaired ETH funding tape
  audited [here](../FUNDING_DIRECTION_SOURCE_SCOUT.md). Funding observations
  have nominal UTC eight-hour `slot_ms` and actual `stamp_ms`. At each
  calendar day `D` use only that day's **00:00** funding record, which the
  archive timestamps at most milliseconds after 00:00. Decision/fill occurs
  at `D 01:00` after a full hour delay, never at the settlement timestamp.
- Define the comparison set as all ETH funding rates with `slot_ms` in
  `[D 00:00 - 180 days, D 00:00)`. Require at least 300 records and a
  complete expected eight-hour cadence in that window. For current rate
  `f`, compute `p = (count(prior < f) + 0.5*count(prior == f))/N`.
  Target `+1` if `p <= 0.10`, otherwise `0`. **Do not additionally require
  f < 0**: the paper's rule is a relative lower decile, not an absolute
  negative-rate filter. No fitted coefficient, magnitude weighting, price
  filter, or side inversion.
- Target at `D 01:00` applies until `(D+1) 01:00`. Use the open price of
  those two completed-source hourly bars as an optimistic executable proxy.
  The 01:00 bar open cannot enter the 00:00 funding percentile. If either
  bar, current settlement or any exposed funding slot is missing, mark
  unresolved; do not substitute cash or interpolate. Exposed funding drag
  is target times actual rates with `stamp_ms` in `(entry, exit]`.
- Start each scored partition flat. Charge `side_bps * abs(target - prior)`
  per day and a final flattening charge on the partition's last scored day;
  5 bp per side primary and 10 bp per side stress. A continued long has no
  new fee; a cash day has zero P&L. Maximum notional is 1x, no leverage or
  interim stop. Score simple `target*(next_open/open - 1)` gross and subtract
  signed funding plus actual target-change cost. Partition by entry date.
  For a conditional 2025 score, stop entries on **December 30**, exiting at
  December 31 01:00, so no 2026 price is read.
