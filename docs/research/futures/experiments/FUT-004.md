Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_BASIS_OUTCOMES`, 2026-09-24. No basis-ranked future return, portfolio PnL, side slice or asset contribution has been inspected. ## Primary question and cheap falsifier

Does a high prior-day perpetual premium over same-name spot predict higher **next-day perpetual** returns than a low premium across a broad point-in-time universe? The primary gross statistic is the equal-weight long top-quartile minus short bottom-quartile daily open-to-next-open return, with 50% gross weight on each side and no spot position. For 2022 and 2023 discovery, the predeclared cheap pass requires **positive annual mean daily gross in each year** and a positive lower endpoint of a 95% calendar-month block-bootstrap interval for their combined daily mean. A nonpositive mean in either year or a nonpositive combined lower endpoint fails the gross-information gate. If selected exposures have unresolved exits, status is `INCOMPLETE_DATA` until they are bounded; a matched-only score cannot pass or fail the gate.

If and only if the gross discovery gate passes, score 2024 as a separate replication and then 2025 as a reserved chronological follow-up, without changing this contract. The 2026 Binance final holdout is untouched. These years were previously inspected for other strategies, so the 2024/2025 observations are not a pristine research program holdout. A viable result still requires an independent venue and prospective shadow at the intended Hyperliquid execution venue before promotion.

## Frozen feature, ranking, and portfolio

- For a UTC entry day `d`, use only the **30 consecutive completed UTC days ending at `d-1`** in both spot and USD-M perpetual daily archives. Exact same-name USDT pairs only; no inferred multiplier, renamed-asset or index mapping. Each prior bar must have a complete daily interval, positive OHLC, positive quote and base volume, and valid continuity. Mean quote turnover over those 30 prior days must be at least USDT 5 million **in each market**. Require complete historical perpetual funding cadence over the same prior window. This is the hashed return-free eligibility contract, including its daily list; do not project today's symbols backward.
- At the prior day close, compute `log(perpetual_close / spot_close)`. Rank all eligible names from low to high, breaking exact ties by symbol text. Require at least 20 eligible names. Select `floor(N/4)` on each tail, with equal weight `+0.5/k` for the highest `k` and `-0.5/k` for the lowest `k`; all other weights are zero. No sign threshold, volatility scaling, price-ratio exclusion, coin/sector subset, horizon change, or ex-post weighting. The four source-QA ratio extremes remain in the causal universe if their prior histories pass; report their influence rather than silently removing them.
- Enter at the next UTC daily perpetual open, hold until the following UTC daily open, and rebalance daily. This next-open proxy has optimistic near-zero decision latency; even a positive gross result cannot establish executable fills. Before promotion, test a delayed fill from timestamped intraday data. A missing or inactive attempted entry is rejected to cash with its fixed weight slice **not** redistributed. A held position with no next open requires a bounded venue settlement and funding outcome, including adverse/favorable 5% stress; never drop it from the score or assume zero return.
- Record daily gross return, number of eligible/ranked/admitted names, exposure, long and short contributions, asset concentration, turnover, monthly and quarterly results, calendar-month bootstrap, rejected orders, forced exits and every unresolved exposure. Use a fixed bootstrap seed `20260924` with 10,000 month resamples. Carry the previous executed weights into the next day's turnover; do not charge a fresh full round trip for an unchanged position. Charge 20bps and 40bps turnover sensitivities plus actual timestamped funding. These historical bps are stress assumptions, not verified account-tier execution costs. Report cash alongside the factor.
