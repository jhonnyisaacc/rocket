## Bound and decision rule frozen before OOS access

For each cessation, use the official dated notice cutoff and a second cutoff at the end of the last positive-volume trade minute when that minute is at or after the notice cutoff. Evaluate every whole-minute cutoff between those endpoints. Within each applicable 30- or 60-minute price-index window, take the mean minute low and mean minute high; use the smallest low mean and largest high mean over cutoff choices. This is a conditional bound on the unpublished second-level settlement average. Use the notice cutoff and mean minute close for the middle scenario. Include any funding settlement timestamped between the earliest and latest cutoff on whichever side is adverse/favorable to the held position. Charge an intraday exit under the original 20/40bps turnover cost. Keep a next-day order with no valid open in cash without reallocating its planned weight. Add a separate 5% price stress beyond both ends of the already expanded price range for every cessation; it is a robustness check, not a claimed exchange price.

The OOS cheap gate is negative for this exact base strategy if its **favorable 5% stress** still has nonpositive 2025 mean daily net return at 20bps plus funding. A positive middle result that fails the adverse stress is data-sensitive and cannot be promoted. The 40bps, 60-day control and cash are reported beside the primary. This rule was written before the scenario code was asked to summarize any 2025 OOS return.

The frozen [FUT-001 contract](FUT-001.md) produced 384 exposed outcome issues across the primary and control, all involving 70 symbols around contract cessation. The settlement candidate builder associated those 70 symbol/date events with official Binance notice leads and checksum-verified minute trade and index archives. All 70 have an eligible entry on the cessation day and complete funding coverage from that day's open to the assumed settlement cutoff. During the 2023–2024 discovery period, 35 such positions are closed intraday at the candidate settlement price and 35 next-morning orders receive no fill; the unfilled target weight remains cash. Settlement also adds exit turnover under the frozen 20/40bps round-trip cost model. [Binance's delisting rules](https://www.binance.com/en-BH/support/faq/detail/dd60dfbf654d4055aa6b217ea6d5ddba) say automatic settlement incurs the taker fee. The baseline cost scenario covers the modeled exit turnover; actual account-tier fees remain to be checked before acceptance.

The adverse/favorable prices select the mean of each minute's low/high against the position side over the applicable 30- or 60-minute window. If an archive has trades at or after the announced cutoff, the range also covers every whole-minute cutoff from the notice time through the end of the last active trade minute (at most three minutes later in this set). Funding payments recorded in that cutoff interval are bounded against the position side; ALPACAUSDT has one such payment, stamped three milliseconds after 09:00 UTC on 2025-04-30. These are sensitivity endpoints **conditional** on the minute index OHLC encompassing every second-level observation. Binance specifies an average of second-level index prices, which the public minute archive does not directly provide. Ten of 70 candidate events have positive-volume trade minutes after their assumed cutoff, including one discovery event (OCEANUSDT), and require source investigation. [Binance's OCEAN/AGIX notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/e485541c370240adba3929fded74f269) specifies 09:00 UTC for both contracts; the checked OCEAN minute archive nevertheless records six positive-volume trades at 09:01 UTC. Its multi-horizon weight was −0.000768. Even replacing its favorable-range exit with a zero settlement price, the maximum gain on that single short position cannot offset the approximately 0.097 sum of 729 discovery daily net returns at 20bps. This protects the negative discovery sign against that one discrepancy; it does not validate the other settlement estimates. Group notice publication times and affected contracts still need final audit. No statistic above validates a settlement price or licenses the 2025 gate.
