# FUT-008: near-touch sell pressure versus best-bid capacity

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_PRESSURE_CONDITIONED_RETURNS`, 2026-09-24.
This is an optimistic, short-only **OKX venue-transfer pilot** of the
mechanism in [Chang (2026)](https://ssrn.com/abstract=6693260), not a
replication of its regression and not a Hyperliquid execution test. The
paper's public abstract reports that recent sell pressure relative to
best-bid depth predicts lower short-horizon BTC perpetual returns and
passive-buy toxicity. It gives no executable rule or costed effect size.
The [source audit](../ORDERBOOK_SOURCE_SCOUT.md) established reconstructable
400-level OKX books and UTC+8 trade-file boundaries without inspecting
pressure-conditioned returns.

## Mechanism and cheap falsifier

Recent aggressive net selling that exceeds the current displayed best-bid
queue may exhaust immediate absorption and precede a further fall. It may
also be a short-lived reaction already priced by the time an order arrives.
Test a **short** for exactly five minutes when the preceding one-minute net
taker-sell quantity exceeds the best-bid displayed quantity. Cash
otherwise. Do not invert the sign, add a long leg, select a volatility
state, or tune the ratio after seeing results.

First score **2023-04-01** and **2024-09-01** separately. They were fixed
as early and later source-audit days before this signal was defined. Both
are before the paper's May 2026 publication, so a pass is a venue-transfer
pilot, not post-publication validation. The primary gate is:

- At least **20 eligible triggered intervals on each day**. Otherwise mark
  `NO_SAMPLE`; an isolated-day study cannot support promotion.
- On **each** day, positive mean triggered short mid-to-mid gross return,
  positive triggered-minus-all-interval short mid-to-mid gross difference,
  and positive mean triggered short bid-to-ask return after **5 bp per
  executed side**.
- Combined triggered mean bid-to-ask return remains positive after **10 bp
  per side**. A pass only warrants acquiring a continuous multiweek source
  window and conditional 2025 replication with this unchanged rule. It does
  not promote a component or strategy.

If either day fails a relevant gate, close this exact five-minute taker
short rule. Do not rescue it by selecting a day, changing threshold,
lengthening the hold, assuming maker fills, or treating the paper's
passive-buy toxicity result as directional P&L. Report both days, counts,
signal ratios, execution gross, costs, and controls even on failure. The
fixed **2025-01-01** L2 sample is source-audited but its
pressure-conditioned outcomes are reserved; 2026 remains untouched.

## Exact causal schedule

- Instrument: `BTC-USDT-SWAP` on OKX only. A 0.01 BTC contract face value
  is supported by OKX's published materials, but the signal is a ratio of
  contract quantities and does not require that conversion. The field
  `side` is treated as the aggressor side, consistent with [OKX public
  trade documentation](https://www.okx.com/docs-v5/en/). No other asset or
  present-day contract universe enters this pilot.
- Decision times `T`: every UTC five-minute boundary from **00:05** through
  **23:50**, inclusive (286 nonoverlapping scheduled windows per day).
  Require all trades with exchange `created_time` in `[T-60s, T)` and the
  reconstructed book after the last L2 message with `ts < T`. The book
  message must be no older than **1 second** at `T`. No event at 00:00 or
  23:55 uses a prior/next calendar day's source.
- Let `S` and `B` be total sell- and buy-taker **contract quantity** in
  that preceding minute. Let `D` be the displayed quantity at the best bid
  just before `T`. Require `D > 0` and a valid positive-spread book. Define
  `pressure = max(0, S-B)/D`; target short iff `pressure > 1` (strict).
  The threshold states the mechanism's absorption claim directly; no
  fitted percentile or lookback optimization is used.
- After a **10-second** processing delay, use the first complete L2 book
  with timestamp at or after `T+10s` and within **1 second** as the
  optimistic entry proxy: short at its best bid. Use the first complete
  book at or after `T+5m+10s`, within **1 second**, to buy back at its
  best ask. If either point is unavailable, crossed, or stale, mark the
  interval unresolved; do not substitute a later price or cash result.
  Use entry/exit mids from those same books for the forecast statistic.
  The next entry is no earlier than the previous exit target clock; every
  triggered interval is flattened, including consecutive triggers.
- Short mid gross is `(entry_mid-exit_mid)/entry_mid`. Short executable
  proxy gross is `(entry_bid-exit_ask)/entry_bid`, so spread is already
  charged. Subtract `2*side_bps/10000` for each fully executed interval,
  with primary `side_bps=5` and stress `side_bps=10`. This is an optimistic
  fill at the displayed touch with no market impact, rejection, funding,
  adverse queue movement or extra latency. Omitting those costs favors the
  hypothesis. No leverage, portfolio throttle, stop or reinvestment.
