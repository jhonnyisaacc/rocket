Status: `FROZEN_UNSCORED`, 2026-09-24. This contract is fixed before
any liquidation-conditioned future price or return is read. The event
state is forced market liquidation, distinct from the voluntary losing
closes rejected by FUT-010 and FUT-011. The 120-minute horizon follows
slide 47 of the [author presentation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qUTWDaL6j-cWBO8myGAg7cGxLjMBLs3C/view);
the displayed markout begins at a distressed fill and does not measure

2025-09-25, and 2025-10-04. The [SHA-256 manifest](../source_manifests/hyperliquid_full_days_2025.json)

timestamp presence in both proposed proxy windows. This is a source

## Causal event and price rule

Partition each full day into UTC five-minute bins `[t,t+5m)`. A bin
qualifies if it contains at least one HYPE **market-method** liquidation
whose fill is a taker close by `liquidatedUser`. Exclude backstop and
unmarked voluntary closes. Do not filter `closedPnl`: forced liquidation
is an account-level state and a position can close at positive realized
P&L. Require a close-long sell to have `side=A` and a close-short buy to
have `side=B`. Compute `S = sum(close-long px*sz) - sum(close-short px*sz)`
in USD over qualifying fills. `S>0` predicts long; `S<0` predicts short;
`S=0` is cash. No size threshold, wallet cohort, or side selection.

Only consider bins with a complete observation interval and with
`[t+126m,t+126m+10s)` inside the same full day. In chronological order,
retain the first time-eligible HYPE bin, then suppress subsequent bins
until 30 minutes after that retained bin. This refractory period is
market-event separation, including cash decisions. Observe fills by
block time, but require local receipt by `t+6m`. Decision and optimistic
entry proxy start at `t+6m`, one minute after the bin ends. Entry is the
size-weighted VWAP of unique HYPE taker trades in
`[t+6m,t+6m+10s)`; exit is the same in
`[t+126m,t+126m+10s)`, exactly 120 minutes later. A unique trade is
`(block_number, coin, tid)` with one taker and one maker wallet fill.
Missing or invalid proxy means cash, with reason counted. Do not
substitute the liquidation print or a later price. Overlapping event
returns are equal-notional diagnostics, not a self-financing portfolio.

For active episodes, gross bp is
`10,000 * direction * (exit_vwap / entry_vwap - 1)`.
Score always-long and always-short **on the same retained bins and proxy
prices**. Report gross less 9 bp (base taker round-trip fee floor) and
gross less 20 bp (10 bp per side stress). Both are optimistic: trade VWAP
does not supply spread, impact, depth or latency; funding is omitted.
