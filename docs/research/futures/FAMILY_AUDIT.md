# Futures family audit

Status: `CONSOLIDATED_NO_ADMISSION`, 2026-09-30. This is the canonical family
decision for PR #38 through FUT-013. Read the [admission gate](EXPERIMENT_ADMISSION.md)
before proposing any successor. Every family has **zero authorized new outcome
trials** today. Source availability, a paper, or a new experiment number grants
no admission. Historical experiment contracts and results remain evidence.

## States and counting

- `REJECTED`: the named implementation or starting architecture is unsuitable
  on its tested sample. This does not reject every idea sharing its vocabulary.
- `CLOSED_EXACT_RULE`: the registered economics or replication gate failed;
  no automatic parameter, subgroup or venue continuation is allowed.
- `OPEN_DATA_GATE`: a materially distinct interpretation remains untested
  because causal source or measurement requirements are unresolved.
- `OPEN_MECHANISM`: an incremental role is untested and has no approved rule.
- `STRUCTURAL_BUT_OUT_OF_CURRENT_PRODUCT_SCOPE`: payoff requires a different
  product and execution architecture; directional experiment registration is denied.
