# Futures data readiness

Status: 2022–2025 raw acquisition and normalization complete; **bounded settlement/outcome reconstruction has produced a provisional FUT-001 OOS score**, while exact second-level settlement prices and some late-trade cases remain unaudited. This page records the data gate and its remaining limits.

## Source and observed checks (2026-09-24)

- [Binance's public-data repository](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/README.md) documents downloadable USD-M futures monthly/daily kline archives, column meanings, and `.CHECKSUM` sidecars. A read-only probe of the underlying public archive listing at `https://s3-ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/data.binance.vision` returned XML for `data/futures/um/monthly/klines/`, including separate contract directories. This can enumerate names that a current exchange-info response omits. The listing is **file availability**, not proof of listing/delisting dates or tradability.
- A full read-only inventory at 2026-09-24T14:25:43Z returned **1,018 distinct monthly kline directories**, including `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`, `FTTUSDT`, `LUNAUSDT`, and multiplier names. The count is a coverage bound, not 1,018 eligible perpetuals: contract type, valid months, quote asset, first/last actual trade and delisting status still require verification. The untracked JSON output was kept outside the PR because directory names alone are not a research dataset; rerun the catalog to reproduce the count at a later retrieval time.
- A second probe downloaded `BTCUSDT-1d-2020-01.zip`: its SHA-256 matched the published `.CHECKSUM`, and the archive contained 31 daily rows from 2020-01-01 through 2020-01-31 UTC. A separate public `fapi/v1/fundingRate` query for BTCUSDT on 2020-01-01 returned three timestamped rates. These are single-contract feasibility checks, **not** proof of broad archive/funding completeness or correct historical universe membership.
- The public monthly archive also has a `fundingRate/` category. A full directory inventory at 2026-09-24T14:31:47Z found 1,018 candle directories, 952 funding directories, 951 paired directories, and **864 paired USDT names**. Per-symbol file inventory found 81 names with both data types in 2020, 253 in 2023, 384 in 2024, and 612 in 2025; the 2022–2025 acquisition plan has 27,291 ZIP files. This is still file-level coverage, not row-level or PIT eligibility. Inventory SHA-256: `576362d58a6c6b1ca7f5437adada772827dfab44c9aef53aa29bf357c857380e` for the local retrieval JSON.
- Verified raw samples: BTCUSDT January 2020 price and funding ZIPs, LUNAUSDT May 2022 price and funding ZIPs, and FTTUSDT June 2025 price and funding ZIPs all matched source checksums. LUNAUSDT's final sampled day has zero volume and flat OHLC; FTTUSDT's sampled candle file continues after its last funding row. These concrete cases require row-level eligibility, gap checks and unresolved-outcome handling. A 52-file 2022 BTC/LUNA/FTT probe passed checksum acquisition; normalization retained 48 zero-volume/flat bars as ineligible rather than deleting them.

It retained 1,144 inactive price bars, admitted BTC and ETH only in the 2023–2025 signal window, and found zero exposed-day gaps on that **small probe**. This validates parser behavior, not the full universe or any strategy result.

- The frozen `FUT-001` causal preflight found **242,588 eligible symbol-days across 517 symbols**, but **384 exposed outcome issues across the primary and 60-day control** (192 per component). They include inactive/missing next opens and settlement/funding boundary cases. The preflight produced no performance table. Several issue dates coincide with contract cessations, so excluding them or treating flat daily bars as fills would bias the result. That initial preflight verdict was `INCOMPLETE_DATA`; the later bounded scenario is linked below.
- [Binance's April 2025 delisting notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/fac9c3e401da4cc8b604566fd261d70c) says affected futures were automatically settled at 09:00 UTC on April 14. [Binance's futures delisting rules](https://www.binance.com/en-BH/support/faq/detail/dd60dfbf654d4055aa6b217ea6d5ddba) specify automatic settlement and a historical settlement-price calculation based on the price index. [The November 2024 rule change](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/4bcabddf0e81423ebca242e185bf157d) shortened the averaging window from 60 to 30 minutes. Checksum-verified BADGERUSDT and PERPUSDT minute trade archives show their last active minute at 09:00 UTC on their respective cessation dates, while later daily rows are flat and zero-volume; corresponding minute index-price archives exist. This is a viable reconstruction route, but the exact settlement time, applicable rule, price approximation and fee must be verified contract by contract before replacing unresolved outcomes.
- A targeted continuation retrieved and checksum-verified the minute trade and index-price ZIPs for **all 78 symbol-month probes** prompted by unresolved exposures, including four prior-month boundary probes. A provisional calendar links **45 dated event groups and 48 notice leads** to **70 symbol/date minute-archive cessation events**. Three contracts on shared dates required their own notices: OMGUSDT, UXLINKUSDT, and KDAUSDT; the first date-level links did not mention them. Every candidate has a complete 30- or 60-minute index-price window under its assumed cutoff; these are averages of **minute closes**, while Binance's published rule calls for an average of **second-level index prices**. The public BADGERUSDT daily index archive advertises 1-minute as its finest interval, with no 1-second directory. Candidate output includes the means of minute low and high as a sensitivity envelope; using it to bound the official average assumes the minute bars capture every second-level index observation. The candidate values are therefore unscored sensitivity inputs, not accepted settlement prices. Ten candidates have a positive-volume trade minute after the assumed cutoff and are flagged for source investigation. The notices still need affected-symbol and publication-time checks for every event, especially grouped notices; the official [PORT3USDT notice](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/announcement/detail/aa3cc74779bf456e8b100304905b9cc2) establishes an exceptional 06:30 UTC cutoff. The retained raw probe and candidate JSON are in ignored local research data, with source keys and SHA-256 hashes; the notice URL leads are tracked in `settlement_notice_candidates.json`.
- PR #31 observed Hyperliquid's roughly 5,000-hour 1h candle limit; PR #32 observed about a year of 1d candles and a current-listed universe missing delistings. Hyperliquid alone cannot presently answer the broad long-history frontier. It remains the intended overlap/forward-shadow venue.
- A separate [provisional 2023–2024 discovery sensitivity](experiments/FUT-001-SETTLEMENT-SCENARIO.md) carries the 35 candidate forced settlements and 35 rejected next-day orders through the frozen FUT-001 weights and costs. It eliminates modeled unresolved exposures and gives a negative multi-horizon discovery mean net return throughout the minute-price sensitivity range. The later [bounded 2025 OOS cheap gate](experiments/FUT-001-OOS.md) found a cost-sensitive multi-horizon result with no promotion; exact settlement-price and late-trade discrepancies remain under audit.
- The official archive kline schema includes open/close time, OHLC, base volume and quote asset volume. Archive completeness, repaired files, anomalous candles, contract type and historical settlement/funding coverage are still unverified. Funding history may need a separate timestamped source; absent funding must remain missing.

## Required acquisition contract before acceptance or promotion

1. Inventory **all** USD-M perpetual contract candidates from historical archive keys, not today's exchange list. Distinguish USDT/USDC quote assets, dated futures, renamed/multiplier contracts and duplicate economic exposure. Preserve raw symbol and mapping history. First/last archive file only bounds coverage; verify first/last actual bar and known listing/delisting records where possible.
2. Download immutable monthly daily bars with source key, retrieval time, checksum/size, dataset version and local SHA-256. Verify source `.CHECKSUM`, parse header variants, timestamps, duplicate/missing intervals, OHLC invariants, positive prices, quote volume, and unexpected discontinuities. Reject or flag rather than silently filling gaps. Keep delisted names and record archive revisions.
3. Define membership at decision time from observed completed bars and lagged rolling quote turnover, a warmup requirement and explicit availability; do not assume an archive directory or a future bar implies earlier tradability. Avoid today's top-100 or current market-cap ranks. Conservative first-bar and stale-data rules must be frozen before PnL.
4. Acquire historical funding settlements with timestamp and source. Audit coverage, interval changes, missing hours, and mapping to held positions. Build costs from explicit fee, spread and slippage scenarios; assess capacity from lagged turnover. Missing funding cannot be zero-filled. Test more severe cost cases.
5. Freeze chronological discovery/OOS windows and a final untouched holdout before signal outcomes. Keep asset and calendar-period breakdown and account for overlapping outcomes. Hash the accepted normalized tape and specification.
6. Only after the base signal survives on Binance, check Hyperliquid overlap with its own symbol map, prices, funding and venue costs. A Binance result does not establish Hyperliquid execution performance.
