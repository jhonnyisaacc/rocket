## What was incomplete architecturally

Main combines momentum eligibility, macro/COT and liquidity in a final
candidate funnel. Its strict six-bar structure/retest and staged retracement
states are workflow outputs, not scientifically separated outcomes. Research
scripts mixed overlapping event means, portfolio accounting and timing gates.
Historical papers/positive slices sometimes motivated the next variant before
a family-level stop. #38 repaired much of that with ledgers and admission, but
its deterministic strategy scorers do not constitute a rare-event outcome,
conditional forecast or mature prospective evidence layer.

Main has useful PointInTime eligibility, read-only providers, human gates,
UNKNOWN semantics and source provenance. ResearchStore journals immutable run
IDs by convention but uses replace-based writes and mutable latest/context
pointers; it does not enforce forecast immutability, schema identity or separate
future enrichment. The new journal uses DB constraints/triggers, integrity
hashes and separate tables, leaving operational storage intact.

The new momentum path is `rocket.momentum`: actual/reconstructed clocks →
low-dimensional snapshots → deterministic crossings → future-only labels →
non-predictive census → gated conditional trial. Label and diagnostic episode
files cannot enter collect.py; no LLM, old trading policy or live context is
called during historical replay. No giant strategy framework or second live
canonical scan is introduced.
