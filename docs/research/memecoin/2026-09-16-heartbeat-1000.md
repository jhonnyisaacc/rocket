## Data-quality finding

The Fomo SCRIBE tab was not present in the current browser inventory. This is not evidence that SCRIBE changed; it is evidence that browser UI state is not a reliable continuous-monitoring substrate. A final alert product therefore needs a durable event collector and immutable snapshots, with Fomo used as enrichment/validation rather than the primary clock.

This reinforces the earlier observation that the Fomo dashboard changed materially within minutes. A signal engine cannot reconstruct an alert from a later screen: it must persist the exact observations available at the trigger time.

## Product implication

The alert pipeline needs two timestamps for every field:

- `event_time`: when the on-chain/social event occurred;
- `available_at`: when the monitor could have known it.

The alert record also needs `observed_at`, `alert_at`, and `expires_at`. Browser-derived fields should be labeled `observed_ui`, carry a freshness timeout, and never silently substitute for missing on-chain data.
