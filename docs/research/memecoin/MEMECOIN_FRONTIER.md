# Memecoin Frontier

Status: current, 2026-09-24. One live question; update after each accepted or rejected experiment.

**Question:** Which features genuinely observable during the earliest token lifecycle contain information about later economically tradable outcomes after realistic execution?

## Current evidence and bottleneck

MC-001 established a reachable native Solana Pump log stream and measured a bounded prospective session: 2,458/2,458 signatures matched the independent index in fully observed interior slots; six CreateEvents and 437 TradeEvents decoded with the pinned official IDL; five create mints matched fetched transactions; 113/113 native non-Mayhem state transitions and 208/208 integer trade quotes reconciled. The first/last slots were excluded. This proves a viable read-only *research* acquisition path for that window. It does not establish a complete long-running panel, exact subsecond latency, inclusion/fill feasibility or tradable alpha. `main` still has no continuous strategy collector. See [MC-001](experiments/MC-001-RESULT.md).

## Current falsifier and next step

MC-002 extended the covered stream to 180 seconds: 37,557/37,557 inner-slot signatures matched, 104 creates and 9,253 trades decoded, and 4,682/4,682 supported new-mint state transitions reconciled. The [frozen baseline result](experiments/MC-002-RESULT.md) left only 19 costed quotes from 104 launches, with three in its temporal evaluation slice. Twelve exits lacked sellable quote liquidity and ten entries lacked an as-of fee state. The small quoted subset and its positive two-name top-quartile evaluation mean cannot support an edge. The development top quartile was worse than all quoted names.

[MC-003](experiments/MC-003-RESULT.md) applied the same frozen rank and costs to a new 300-second cohort. An independent second-provider index matched 38,941/38,941 interior signatures; 102 creates and 12,901 trades decoded. Of 52 eligible launches, 40 had quoted exits and 12 lacked sellable exit liquidity. The held-out quoted mean was **−8.99%** across 11 names; the top curve-progress quartile was **−8.05%** across three. With the frozen zero-recovery exit stress, held-out means were **−33.67%** across all 15 scored names and **−31.42%** across the top four. Development's top quartile was worse than the full group. These results reject the unchanged five-second curve-progress rank as an entry basis under this cost scenario. The stress is a scenario, not observed liquidation.

The next falsifiable question is whether genuinely early survival and manipulation-risk observations, coupled with transaction-level inclusion and exit feasibility, can improve net outcomes on new covered time windows. Acquire longer prospective panels and verify transaction balance deltas, observed entry failure, fees and exit routes before specifying that test. Preserve all unknown and unavailable outcomes. Do not retune the failed curve-progress cutoff on MC-002/MC-003.

[MC-004](experiments/MC-004-RESULT.md) calibrated one part of that gate against 56 hash-selected MC-003 transaction records: all were retrieved and matched stream identity/slot. The median whole-transaction fee was 117,500 lamports for sampled single buys and 100,000 for sampled single sells, with 15/48 above the earlier 155,000-lamport per-leg scenario and large outliers. Token-account deltas were consistent with event amounts in 47/48 single-event transactions; the remaining buy created its mint in the same transaction and lacked a pre-balance. This does not measure a hypothetical Rocket order's inclusion or exit route. The next fresh panel must collect those facts and test prespecified survival/risk features, rather than further score tuning on MC-002/MC-003.

[MC-005](experiments/MC-005-RESULT.md) tested a frozen five-second net trade-flow count on a fresh 600-second panel. The second-provider index matched 152,120/152,120 interior signatures and the corrected pinned-protocol audit reconciled 23,910 supported quotes. The top quartile had fewer unavailable curve exits in both temporal splits (1/25 development and 0/11 evaluation) than the remaining entry-known names (19/57 and 14/31), making early flow a **candidate exit-risk factor**. Its economic entry result is not robust: development top stress means were negative, and the positive held-out means under two fee scenarios turned negative when one modeled +288.65% winner was removed. Create-event receipt delays also reached more than ten seconds for 28/251 launches, clustered in one interval. No entry rule is promoted.

The next falsifier is an **unchanged risk definition on another independently covered time window**, with measured receipt delays and transaction-level participant/exit-route truth. Test whether early flow distinguishes genuinely independent buyers from churn and whether a curve-unavailable sell has a usable alternate venue. Keep economic and catastrophic-risk outcomes separate; use cost and latency stress before accepting any rank.

[MC-006](experiments/MC-006-RESULT.md) ran that unchanged risk definition on a second ten-minute prospective window. Its independent index matched 199,403/199,403 interior signatures and the protocol audit passed 18,632 supported quotes. Top flow names again had lower observed curve-exit unavailability than the rest (development 4/26 versus 22/75; evaluation 0/11 versus 6/32), but exact intervals were broad. Their economic stress means were negative in both splits under both fee scenarios. The v2 collector reduced disk syncs sharply, yet CreateEvent receipt-age p90 was 28.1 seconds; the fast-discovery gate failed. An alternate public websocket failed independent coverage, missing 466 of 871 interior signatures in a short test. MC-004's transaction check also found one routed sell whose `TradeEvent.user` was an intermediary rather than the token-source owner.

The next bottleneck is **actionable acquisition and actor/route truth**. Benchmark another stream or ingestion path with simultaneous receive clocks and independent signature coverage before spending another holdout on ranking. Then verify whether the flow risk association survives fast receipt, distinct beneficial buyers and route-specific exit accounting. The two prospective risk associations justify this targeted test, not a live entry rule.

[MC-007](experiments/MC-007-RESULT.md) simultaneously compared the two public Solana websocket aliases for 120 seconds. On 11,831 common signatures, receipt times differed by only 13 milliseconds at p90 and 108 milliseconds at p99; both had about 1.5-second CreateEvent receipt-age p90. Yet both feeds emitted the exact same all-ones signature absent from the independent chain index and later transaction query. Each failed the prespecified exact-coverage screen. This short fast interval does not resolve the 10–45-second bursts in the longer MC-005/MC-006 windows, and the second alias is not a validated substitute.

The next acquisition test should use a **dedicated independently indexed stream or Geyser path** with full raw receipts, transaction identity checks, and a window long enough to assess burst incidence. Then return to actor/exit-route semantics and only then spend another untouched strategy holdout. Preserve the anomalous frame as a data-quality failure; do not silently exclude it to pass coverage.

[MC-008](experiments/MC-008-RESULT.md) tested a full-transaction block feed as another way through the data barrier. It exactly matched 7,481/7,481 independent interior transaction signatures and retained signed transaction bytes, yet CreateEvent age p90 was 10.872 seconds. Twenty-nine successful transactions had truncated logs, and two reserve-continuity gaps exposed missing trade events after truncation. Thus transaction coverage alone cannot certify the early feature stream. The block feed is useful for transaction-level audit/backfill, but fails the fast and complete-lifecycle gates for strategy evaluation.

The next test should pair the fastest independently auditable **creation feed** with transaction-level reconciliation of its full new-launch universe. Probe a dedicated Geyser/Yellowstone route if access is available; also test any accessible real-time creation feed against chain signatures and receive clocks. Require a longer observation window and explicitly mark truncated histories and unknown exits. The simple net-flow rank stays frozen and unpromoted until the acquisition and actor/route gates are met.

[MC-009](experiments/MC-009-RESULT.md) paired a third-party creation feed with the official Solana log stream for three minutes. PumpPortal was 182 milliseconds earlier at the median among 44 matched launches, but it missed 14/58 native Pump creates in the frozen core; 13 of 43 strict native non-Mayhem launches were absent. Seven additional notifications were valid Bonk transactions outside the Pump program. The official log arm had full independent chain signatures plus the recurring all-zeros-signature artifact, so its raw exact-coverage gate also failed. Neither feed alone establishes a complete early Pump universe. A public dRPC websocket capability probe returned “method is not available on free plan,” and Ankr rejected an unauthenticated websocket; these probes are not cohort evidence.

The immediate next falsifier is a **prospectively frozen valid-transaction quarantine rule** on a new, longer Solana log panel. Preserve and count all malformed frames, but admit only independently indexed, structurally valid signed transactions to point-in-time features. Require zero missing valid chain signatures and transaction-confirmed creation identity. Measure the proportion of launches whose complete early feature window actually arrives within five seconds; late launches remain in the denominator and become no-decision cases. In parallel, a dedicated authenticated stream should be benchmarked if access becomes available. Any strategy baseline must use a fresh covered cohort and the frozen time/fee/exit rules, not MC-009's acquisition window.

## Decision today

Status `OPEN`: full-window early event acquisition is technically viable on the tested primary stream, but episodic 10–45-second receipt age blocks uniform early actionability. The block feed has complete transaction signatures in its tested minute but arrives about ten seconds late and has truncated logs. The simple curve-progress entry rank is rejected; net trade flow is a candidate **exit-risk** feature but not an accepted economic entry rule. Executable exits and a profitable strategy remain unproven. No model or signal is promoted to Rocket decisions.
