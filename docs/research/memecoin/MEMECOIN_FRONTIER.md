# Memecoin Frontier

Status: current, 2026-09-24. One live question; update after each accepted or rejected experiment.

**Question:** Which features genuinely observable during the earliest token lifecycle contain information about later economically tradable outcomes after realistic execution?

## Current evidence and bottleneck

MC-001 established a reachable native Solana Pump log stream and measured a bounded prospective session: 2,458/2,458 signatures matched the independent index in fully observed interior slots; six CreateEvents and 437 TradeEvents decoded with the pinned official IDL; five create mints matched fetched transactions; 113/113 native non-Mayhem state transitions and 208/208 integer trade quotes reconciled. The first/last slots were excluded. This proves a viable read-only *research* acquisition path for that window. It does not establish a complete long-running panel, exact subsecond latency, inclusion/fill feasibility or tradable alpha. `main` still has no continuous strategy collector. See [MC-001](experiments/MC-001-RESULT.md).

## Current falsifier and next step

MC-002 extended the covered stream to 180 seconds: 37,557/37,557 inner-slot signatures matched, 104 creates and 9,253 trades decoded, and 4,682/4,682 supported new-mint state transitions reconciled. The [frozen baseline result](experiments/MC-002-RESULT.md) left only 19 costed quotes from 104 launches, with three in its temporal evaluation slice. Twelve exits lacked sellable quote liquidity and ten entries lacked an as-of fee state. The small quoted subset and its positive two-name top-quartile evaluation mean cannot support an edge. The development top quartile was worse than all quoted names.

[MC-003](experiments/MC-003-RESULT.md) applied the same frozen rank and costs to a new 300-second cohort. An independent second-provider index matched 38,941/38,941 interior signatures; 102 creates and 12,901 trades decoded. Of 52 eligible launches, 40 had quoted exits and 12 lacked sellable exit liquidity. The held-out quoted mean was **−8.99%** across 11 names; the top curve-progress quartile was **−8.05%** across three. With the frozen zero-recovery exit stress, held-out means were **−33.67%** across all 15 scored names and **−31.42%** across the top four. Development's top quartile was worse than the full group. These results reject the unchanged five-second curve-progress rank as an entry basis under this cost scenario. The stress is a scenario, not observed liquidation.

The next falsifiable question is whether genuinely early survival and manipulation-risk observations, coupled with transaction-level inclusion and exit feasibility, can improve net outcomes on new covered time windows. Acquire longer prospective panels and verify transaction balance deltas, observed entry failure, fees and exit routes before specifying that test. Preserve all unknown and unavailable outcomes. Do not retune the failed curve-progress cutoff on MC-002/MC-003.

[MC-004](experiments/MC-004-RESULT.md) calibrated one part of that gate against 56 hash-selected MC-003 transaction records: all were retrieved and matched stream identity/slot. The median whole-transaction fee was 117,500 lamports for sampled single buys and 100,000 for sampled single sells, with 15/48 above the earlier 155,000-lamport per-leg scenario and large outliers. Token-account deltas were consistent with event amounts in 47/48 single-event transactions; the remaining buy created its mint in the same transaction and lacked a pre-balance. This does not measure a hypothetical Rocket order's inclusion or exit route. The next fresh panel must collect those facts and test prespecified survival/risk features, rather than further score tuning on MC-002/MC-003.

[MC-005](experiments/MC-005-RESULT.md) tested a frozen five-second net trade-flow count on a fresh 600-second panel. The second-provider index matched 152,120/152,120 interior signatures and the corrected pinned-protocol audit reconciled 23,910 supported quotes. The top quartile had fewer unavailable curve exits in both temporal splits (1/25 development and 0/11 evaluation) than the remaining entry-known names (19/57 and 14/31), making early flow a **candidate exit-risk factor**. Its economic entry result is not robust: development top stress means were negative, and the positive held-out means under two fee scenarios turned negative when one modeled +288.65% winner was removed. Create-event receipt delays also reached more than ten seconds for 28/251 launches, clustered in one interval. No entry rule is promoted.

The next falsifier is an **unchanged risk definition on another independently covered time window**, with measured receipt delays and transaction-level participant/exit-route truth. Test whether early flow distinguishes genuinely independent buyers from churn and whether a curve-unavailable sell has a usable alternate venue. Keep economic and catastrophic-risk outcomes separate; use cost and latency stress before accepting any rank.

[MC-006](experiments/MC-006-RESULT.md) ran that unchanged risk definition on a second ten-minute prospective window. Its independent index matched 199,403/199,403 interior signatures and the protocol audit passed 18,632 supported quotes. Top flow names again had lower observed curve-exit unavailability than the rest (development 4/26 versus 22/75; evaluation 0/11 versus 6/32), but exact intervals were broad. Their economic stress means were negative in both splits under both fee scenarios. The v2 collector reduced disk syncs sharply, yet CreateEvent receipt-age p90 was 28.1 seconds; the fast-discovery gate failed. An alternate public websocket failed independent coverage, missing 466 of 871 interior signatures in a short test. MC-004's transaction check also found one routed sell whose `TradeEvent.user` was an intermediary rather than the token-source owner.

The next bottleneck is **actionable acquisition and actor/route truth**. Benchmark another stream or ingestion path with simultaneous receive clocks and independent signature coverage before spending another holdout on ranking. Then verify whether the flow risk association survives fast receipt, distinct beneficial buyers and route-specific exit accounting. The two prospective risk associations justify this targeted test, not a live entry rule.

[MC-007](experiments/MC-007-RESULT.md) simultaneously compared the two public Solana websocket aliases for 120 seconds. On 11,831 common signatures, receipt times differed by only 13 milliseconds at p90 and 108 milliseconds at p99; both had about 1.5-second CreateEvent receipt-age p90. Yet both feeds emitted the exact same all-ones signature absent from the independent chain index and later transaction query. Each failed the prespecified exact-coverage screen. This short fast interval does not resolve the 10–45-second bursts in the longer MC-005/MC-006 windows, and the second alias is not a validated substitute.

The next acquisition test should use a **dedicated independently indexed stream or Geyser path** with full raw receipts, transaction identity checks, and a window long enough to assess burst incidence. Then return to actor/exit-route semantics and only then spend another untouched strategy holdout. Preserve the anomalous frame as a data-quality failure; do not silently exclude it to pass coverage.

[MC-008](experiments/MC-008-RESULT.md) tested a full-transaction block feed as another way through the data barrier. It exactly matched 7,481/7,481 independent interior transaction signatures and retained signed transaction bytes, yet CreateEvent age p90 was 10.872 seconds. Twenty-nine successful transactions had truncated logs, and two reserve-continuity gaps exposed missing trade events after truncation. Thus transaction coverage alone cannot certify the early feature stream. The block feed is useful for transaction-level audit/backfill, but fails the fast and complete-lifecycle gates for strategy evaluation.

The next test should pair the fastest independently auditable **creation feed** with transaction-level reconciliation of its full new-launch universe. Probe a dedicated Geyser/Yellowstone route if access is available; also test any accessible real-time creation feed against chain signatures and receive clocks. Require a longer observation window and explicitly mark truncated histories and unknown exits. The simple net-flow rank stays frozen and unpromoted until the acquisition and actor/route gates are met.

[MC-009](experiments/MC-009-RESULT.md) paired a third-party creation feed with the official Solana log stream for three minutes. PumpPortal was 182 milliseconds earlier at the median among 44 matched launches, but it missed 14/58 native Pump creates in the frozen core; 13 of 43 strict native non-Mayhem launches were absent. Seven additional notifications were valid Bonk transactions outside the Pump program. The official log arm had full independent chain signatures plus the recurring all-zeros-signature artifact, so its raw exact-coverage gate also failed. Neither feed alone establishes a complete early Pump universe. A public dRPC websocket capability probe returned “method is not available on free plan,” and Ankr rejected an unauthenticated websocket; these probes are not cohort evidence.

The immediate next falsifier is a **prospectively frozen valid-transaction quarantine rule** on a new, longer Solana log panel. Preserve and count all malformed frames, but admit only independently indexed, structurally valid signed transactions to point-in-time features. Require zero missing valid chain signatures and transaction-confirmed creation identity. Measure the proportion of launches whose complete early feature window actually arrives within five seconds; late launches remain in the denominator and become no-decision cases. In parallel, a dedicated authenticated stream should be benchmarked if access becomes available. Any strategy baseline must use a fresh covered cohort and the frozen time/fee/exit rules, not MC-009's acquisition window.

[MC-010](experiments/MC-010-RESULT.md) met its ten-minute acquisition gate: 154,144/154,144 valid interior signatures matched an independent index; zero frames required structural quarantine, and no truncated logs, decoder errors or native state/quote failures appeared. All 310 creates were at most five seconds old by the coarse integer event timestamp at receipt. Of 240 analytically eligible launches, 238 passed the frozen strict signed-create identity check; two routed or compound creations remain unresolved and excluded. The unchanged top-flow quartile again had fewer unavailable curve exits than the rest in both development (7/42 versus 35/88) and evaluation (1/18 versus 11/33). Yet its zero-recovery stress means were negative in both splits at both fee scenarios, and held-out leave-best-out remained negative. This is a third directional risk association, **not** an economically viable entry rule under the prespecified proxy.

The next falsifier should measure **who actually buys and how positions can exit**. Reconcile early trade participants with signed transaction account owners and funding paths; quantify unique beneficial buyers, routed churn, and concentration without counting intermediaries as independent demand. Separately price and verify alternate PumpSwap or other usable exit routes for curve-unavailable names, and model actual inclusion, priority fees and fill delay for hypothetical Rocket orders. Keep their transaction retrieval clocks later than original event receipt. Acquire another independent fast window or benchmark a dedicated authenticated stream to test whether MC-010's favorable receipt age persists through burst conditions. Freeze each target, sampling rule and outcome before inspecting a new cohort.

[MC-011](experiments/MC-011-RESULT.md) froze a hash-stratified transaction check of MC-010's original five-second early trades before fetching later metadata. All 144 selected signatures resolved; 142 had a unique event-sized token-account owner, and all 142 owners matched the emitted `TradeEvent.user`. Two buys lacked a matching positive account delta and stayed ambiguous. Forty-four transactions entered Pump through another top-level program; one identified owner differed from the fee payer. This calibrates the emitted-user field in a bounded, already evaluated panel, but does **not** prove distinct beneficial controllers or make later transaction metadata available at the earlier decision. MC-004's routed-sell mismatch also remains a concrete universal-shortcut counterexample.

The next fresh economic experiment needs **point-in-time observable participant structure plus route-aware exit truth**, not another retune of net-flow count. First distinguish signers, funders and common control where the signed records support it; quantify what can actually be known by five seconds. Measure alternate venue state and possible liquidation on the 54 MC-010 names with an unavailable modeled curve exit, while retaining entry-unknown and failed-entry cases. None of those 54 had a same-mint completion or migration event in the covered Pump log panel. Freeze the relevant data and cost rules before a new prospective strategy cohort; keep acquisition latency and burst coverage in the gate.

[MC-012](experiments/MC-012-RESULT.md) made a frozen, later third-party pool lookup for those 54 unavailable names and 54 hash-selected quoted controls. Each unavailable name had only a `pumpfun` pair in the saved response; one control had a PumpSwap pair whose listed creation time followed its frozen exit. The result does not establish complete venue coverage or reconstruct liquidity at 67 seconds. It reduces the plausibility that a readily indexed alternate pool repairs the MC-010 stress cases, while leaving their realized liquidation value unknown.

MC-012 pointed to a **prospective, contemporaneous size-aware exit quote** with preserved receive clock, pool/route provenance and failure responses. A quote source must be validated against on-chain pool reserves and its proposed transaction fees before it can replace a zero-recovery stress assumption. The [acquisition options](ACQUISITION_OPTIONS.md) show the public feed limits; an authenticated route or dedicated RPC credential would permit a stronger benchmark if supplied. Deterministic PumpSwap state/fee decoding and funding-path checks remain open. Any new economic feature requires a new frozen panel and temporal holdout.

[MC-013](experiments/MC-013-RESULT.md) captured a new ten-minute log window and contemporaneous Pump bonding-curve accounts. The independent index matched 208,728/208,728 valid interior signatures; all 550 scheduled account responses for 275 creates decoded and met the frozen timing screen. Later signed transaction retrieval confirmed 134/139 scored creations. Direct account quotes and the unchanged event-state stress matched exactly for 116/131 paired outcomes, while 15 differed. The direct-account top flow stress means were negative in both temporal splits at both network-fee scenarios. Lower modeled curve-exit unavailability for high flow repeated, but the economic entry rule still fails and actual fills remain unobserved.

The next falsifier requires a **new prespecified risk/return feature** with independent buyer or funding evidence available by its decision clock, plus contemporaneous alternate-route depth and a realistic Rocket inclusion/failure model. A fresh temporal holdout must test it without retuning on MC-013. Preserve curve-unavailable inventory and missing route quotes as unknown realized outcomes. The bounded public stream was fast in MC-013, but MC-005/MC-006 bursts still make sustained actionability unproved; benchmark a dedicated authenticated stream if available.

[MC-014](experiments/MC-014-RESULT.md) prospectively tested whether signed early-buyer token-owner evidence could actually arrive by a five-second decision. Its 180-second stream matched 48,270/48,270 independent interior signatures. For the first at most three early buys per native non-Mayhem launch, all 135 selected transactions resolved to a unique signed token owner by the checkpoint across 61 covered launches. All selected owners were distinct within each launch, so a simple first-three unique-owner count only reproduces the capped buy count here. The short-window availability gate passes, but beneficial control, longer-window reliability and incremental economic value remain unproved.

The next economic cohort should freeze a **buyer-size dispersion or concentration feature from signed, timely owners**, retain unknown signed responses and all launches, and compare against the unchanged five-second flow rank under direct Pump account quotes. It must also measure or explicitly leave unknown alternate-route liquidation and hypothetical Rocket inclusion. Do not choose a favorable threshold from MC-014, whose returns were not evaluated. Public read access proved feasible for the selected transactions in this short window; it does not remove the earlier long-window feed bursts.

[MC-015](experiments/MC-015-RESULT.md) ran that frozen buyer-size dispersion test on a fresh covered ten-minute panel. Its independent index matched 127,995/127,995 valid interior signatures, all 532 direct Pump curve reads were timely and decodable, and 390/401 selected early buys had signed token-owner truth by the five-second checkpoint. The new score was available for 132/152 analytically eligible creations after strict later creation identity. Its top quartile had **negative** direct-account costed stress means in both temporal splits at both fee scenarios and was worse than the unchanged flow top on the same score-known subset in evaluation. The feature failed all economic performance gates despite meeting sample minimums. No score threshold is retuned on this cohort.

The active falsifier now shifts to **route-aware execution and common funding**, because multiple early ranks have failed under quote/stress proxies. For curve-unavailable names, record contemporaneous canonical migration and any independently verified alternative pool reserves/fees; where route state is absent, retain liquidation as unknown. Separately measure what a hypothetical Rocket order could actually include and pay at the decision clock. Solana's read-only [simulation RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/simulatetransaction) can check an assembled transaction's program behavior and compute but cannot prove landing or an achieved fill; the [fee structure](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees/fee-structure) depends on message signatures and priority instructions. A dedicated stream or funded simulation setup may be needed for a stronger benchmark. Do not spend another untouched rank holdout without a distinct, prespecified mechanism and these execution truths.

[MC-016](experiments/MC-016-RESULT.md) used the already archived MC-015 signed buys as a cheap, frozen payer-overlap diagnostic. Different timely token owners shared a transaction fee payer in **zero of 123** eligible first-three-buy creations. Owner and fee payer matched in 388/390 timely buys. This narrows one observable payer-overlap form, but cannot identify shared prior funding or beneficial control. Route-aware exits and hypothetical Rocket inclusion remain the nearer economic bottleneck; do not reinterpret MC-015's rejected rank.

[MC-017](experiments/MC-017-RESULT.md) measured that canonical route directly on a new ten-minute panel. The independent index matched 107,302/107,302 valid interior signatures; 558 Pump curve account reads and 169 native canonical-pool reads were timely and complete. All **52/52** names with an unavailable direct Pump curve sell had a null canonical PumpSwap pool in the same-bank confirmed read near receipt +67 seconds and no migration event visible by the read. Three present, fully decoded canonical pools followed matching migration events, but those names' hypothetical Pump entries were already unavailable. This removes a canonical migration rescue from those 52 observed exit snapshots. It leaves other venues, actual inventory recovery and Rocket order inclusion unknown. The observed pools carried positive virtual quote reserves and mixed Token-2022/legacy vaults, requiring versioned route and fee accounting.

The next falsifier is an **actual executable route/inclusion calibration**, not another entry cutoff. MC-018's later DEX Screener sweep listed no noncanonical pool for any of MC-017's 52 depleted exits, but its later and incomplete listing snapshot cannot decide as-of venue availability. MC-019 obtained one-bank fee/pool/vault/mint state for three present canonical pools and found an active 125-bps tier, versus only 25 bps in the global static fields. MC-020 then validated size-aware effective-reserve gross, ceiling-rounded LP/protocol/creator fees and signed pool-vault movements in **29/29** hash-selected landed sells; observed fees varied between 125 and 120 bps across those transactions. Those observations were later than the strategy exit clocks and do not show a Rocket order or independently observed user quote cash. MC-021 supplies the amount-specific unsigned simulation for one reachable venue. For noncanonical venues, acquire an independently verified pool address and same-bank vault state at a frozen clock, or leave cash recovery unknown. A funded, bounded shadow order study or authenticated route source is needed to measure actual inclusion; do not fabricate it from historical observed-buyer fees. Only after those constraints are measured should a distinct prespecified economic mechanism receive an untouched temporal holdout.

[MC-021](experiments/MC-021-RESULT.md) completed the amount-specific unsigned sell test on one present canonical pool. The official SDK built a 10,000,000,000 raw-base-unit sell using one-bank confirmed state; two read-only simulations returned `err: null`, and the follow-up owner's simulated native gain was exactly the SDK net quote minus a 45,000-lamport network fee. This validates one executable program path with a public holder's current inventory. It does not show that Rocket can acquire that inventory by the five-second clock, land a transaction under contention, or achieve the simulated cash result. A prospectively timed bounded shadow-order study with observed failures and post-transaction balances is the next inclusion gate. Do not spend a new rank holdout or promote a signal on this simulation alone.

[MC-022](experiments/MC-022-RESULT.md) added a prospectively timed unsigned Pump **entry** diagnostic on a new 180-second capture. The independent index matched 13,443/13,443 interior signatures; five first eligible native non-Mayhem launches had signed creation identity later confirmed. All five unsigned 0.01-SOL buys reached simulation by the frozen +12-second response gate, four succeeded, and one failed `TooMuchSolRequired` when the required quote rose to 10,532,647 lamports above its 10,100,000-lamport cap across three bank slots. These are read-only outcomes from a public hypothetical payer, not Rocket fills or a broad entry failure rate. The active execution falsifier is larger, prespecified decision-clock samples spanning launch times, sizes and latency; actual inclusion and settlement still require separate authorization or an equivalent source. Pair that work with as-of exit-route evidence. Keep the existing rejected ranks rejected and do not spend a new rank holdout merely because a buy simulated.

## Decision today

Status `OPEN`: MC-010, MC-013, MC-015, MC-017 and MC-022 passed bounded valid-signature and log-completeness checks with fast observed receipts, but earlier panels had episodic 10–45-second age bursts, so uniform actionability remains unproved. The block feed was late and log-truncated. The simple curve-progress entry rank and signed first-three buyer-size dispersion rank are rejected under their costed quote proxies; net trade flow is a repeated candidate **exit-risk** feature whose costed economic tests remain negative. MC-014/015 established five-second signed token-owner availability for most selected first-three buys, but not beneficial independence or incremental economic value. MC-017 found the canonical PumpSwap pool absent at the measured exit snapshots for 52 depleted Pump curves; MC-019/020 established later-bank dynamic fee inputs and observed landed-sell arithmetic on three present pools. MC-021 simulated one inventory-backed sell; MC-022 simulated four of five frozen early buys and recorded one slippage failure. Actual Rocket inclusion remains unmeasured. Noncanonical venues and achieved liquidation remain unknown. A profitable strategy is unproven. No model or signal is promoted to Rocket decisions.
