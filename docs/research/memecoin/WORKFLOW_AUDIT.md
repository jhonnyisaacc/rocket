## Continuation bias finding

**Yes, at the direction level.** The process enforced frozen experiment definitions and preserved unknowns, but allowed each unresolved technical question to become the next authorized task. It repeatedly acknowledged no entry edge while keeping the broad family OPEN. Constitution reopening rules protected particular rejected scores; no family budget, cross-experiment failure accumulation or independent direction review prevented indefinite adjacent calibration. The Frontier's repeated “first resolve execution, then test alpha” sequence became self-renewing.

The earliest plausible review checkpoint was MC-006. A stronger practical stop was MC-010, after fast receipt did not rescue flow. MC-013 materially weakened the measurement-artifact explanation; MC-015 rejected a new nearby feature with adequate frozen sample gates. By that point automatic escalation to routes/fees/simulation had less expected decision value than a cheap distinct-mechanism falsifier. This is a priority judgment using evidence then available, not a claim that those protocol checks were incorrect or useless.

There are six disjoint economic cohort failures/nonrobust outcomes (MC-003/005/006/010/013/015), including three negative flow replications after MC-005. They are distinct launch windows on the same day, not six independent market regimes. MC-015's flow comparison is dependent on its dispersion cohort and counts once. Infrastructure failures MC-007/008/009 are not economic failures. No arbitrary failure-count threshold declares all memecoin research dead: the decision combines replication, direct-state calibration, quoted-only failures, a failed adjacent mechanism and absent new decision value.
