# MC-021 result — unsigned PumpSwap sell simulation

Status: **accepted for one inventory-backed, read-only execution simulation**. The [frozen design and follow-up amendment](MC-021-FROZEN.md), [raw archive](../data/mc021-unsigned-sell-20260924.tar.xz), and [offline audit](../../../../scripts/research/memecoin_amm_unsigned_audit.py) preserve the selected public holder, account banks, unsigned transaction bytes, all RPC requests and responses, and failure responses. This is a feasibility diagnostic selected from current holders; it is not a random sample of hypothetical Rocket orders.

The first attempted 30-transaction batch and subsequent four-transaction batches were rejected by publicnode's one-`getTransaction`-per-batch limit. A separately saved two-transaction probe reproduces the HTTP 400 `-32600` response. Eighteen signed transactions were then read individually from the first six of the latest 20 signatures for each of MC-017's three canonical pools. The fixed selection chose a recent buyer account with 13,868,885,223,737 raw base units at confirmed slot 450155771 and 3,979,466,164 lamports in its owner account at slot 450155818. The failed batch attempts were not preserved at the moment of those attempts; their limit and retry are disclosed here. The successful individual reads and selection account responses are archived.

The pinned official PumpSwap SDK 1.20.0 constructed an unsigned 10,000,000,000 raw-base-unit sell with 1% slippage, 400,000 compute-unit limit, and 100,000 micro-lamports per compute unit. Both simulations used a single-bank input read, then a confirmed `simulateTransaction` with signature verification off and blockhash replacement on. The first returned `err: null` at slot 450156069 and consumed 93,933 units. Because the SDK closed the temporary WSOL quote account, the frozen follow-up added the owner's native account to the response and repeated the unchanged amount and holder. It returned `err: null` at slot 450156207 and consumed 89,591 units. Neither transaction was signed or broadcast.

At the follow-up input bank 450156206, the SDK quoted **4,552,975 lamports gross**, **4,498,338 lamports after protocol fees**, and a **4,453,354-lamport minimum quote**. The simulated user's base account fell by exactly 10,000,000,000 raw units, the pool base vault rose by that amount, and the pool WSOL vault fell by 4,543,869 lamports. The simulated transaction fee was 45,000 lamports, and the owner's native balance increased by **4,453,338 lamports**: exactly SDK net quote minus the transaction fee. The owner gain is 16 lamports below the instruction's minimum quote because the minimum applies before the network fee. The offline audit verifies these identities from the saved raw account bytes and RPC result.

This establishes that the official SDK built one amount-specific sell that the mainnet RPC simulated successfully with real public inventory. It does **not** establish that Rocket could obtain that inventory at a five-second decision clock, land the order, pay that exact fee under contention, or recover this amount in a real settlement. It also says nothing about the 52 MC-017 names without a canonical pool at their exit snapshots. The next read-only step can measure simulation availability, quote drift and failure rates prospectively at fixed decision clocks. Actual inclusion and settlement require a separately authorized funded study or an equivalent authenticated execution source; Rocket execution remains disabled. Keep all prior entry ranks unpromoted and `NO_EDGE_VALIDATED`.
