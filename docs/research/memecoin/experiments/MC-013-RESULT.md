## Frozen flow and cost comparison

The frozen score is five-second observed buys minus sells. The quote model uses 10,000,000 lamports at receipt +7 seconds, a receipt +67-second curve exit, 200 bps slippage per leg and 155,000 or 1,000,000 lamports network fee per leg. Direct state comes from contemporaneous accounts; protocol/creator fee parameters come from the latest event available at the corresponding response. These are **modeled quotes**, not order fills. An unavailable curve sell receives zero recovery only in the stress column; its actual liquidation value and any alternate venue remain unknown.

The account-based top group had fewer curve-unavailable exits than the remainder among known entries in both splits (development 1/24 versus 26/70; evaluation 0/10 versus 11/27), extending the directional **exit-risk association** without establishing independent buyers or positive expectancy.

Neither fee scenario has a positive top stress mean in either split. `ENTER` remains unavailable. Even excluding unavailable exits, direct quoted-only top means at the 155,000-lamport fee were −20.91% over 23 development names and −31.70% over ten evaluation names. This limits the chance that the negative stress result is solely an artifact of the zero-recovery assumption.
