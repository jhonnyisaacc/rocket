# MC-020 result — landed canonical PumpSwap sell accounting

Status: **accepted for bounded observed-sell arithmetic and vault accounting**, with hypothetical Rocket simulation and inclusion still open. The [initial design and two acquisition amendments](MC-020-FROZEN.md) were committed before the corresponding transaction retrievals. The original earliest-after-migration index gate **failed**: two pools exhausted 500 signatures before reaching their MC-017 bank slot and the third received HTTP 429. No transaction was selected from that failed attempt. Its raw pages and failure manifest are retained.

The amended diagnostic used the first 100-signature page for each of the same three MC-017 canonical pools, with one saved successful retry after the 429. From those pages, 94, 100 and 100 signatures were successful and above each pool's MC-017 second-read slot; the first page also contained six failed signatures. The committed `sha256("mc020-v2:" + signature)` rule selected **12 per pool, 36 total**. This is a hash sample **within three recent provider pages**, not a complete migration-to-present transaction index. All 36 selected signatures were retrieved and matched signed transaction signature and slot: 34 under the original version-0 request, and two after separately frozen and timestamped version-1 compatibility reads. The original three `-32015` replies for each version-1 transaction remain in the archive and in the primary retrieval denominator.

The pinned PumpSwap IDL decoded **29 SellEvents** attributable to the selected pools; seven selected transactions had no sell on their selected pool. There were nine, eleven and nine decoded sells across the three pools. Every one of the 29 sells passed all of these checks against its signed transaction:

- `floor(base_amount_in × (raw_quote_reserve + virtual_quote_reserve) / (base_reserve + base_amount_in))` equaled the event's gross `quote_amount_out`.
- LP, protocol and creator fee amounts each equaled `ceil(gross_quote × respective_bps / 10,000)`.
- Gross minus LP fee equaled `quote_amount_out_without_lp_fee`; that amount minus protocol and creator fees equaled event `user_quote_amount_out`.
- The pre-transaction pool base and WSOL vault balances equaled the event's reserve fields. The base vault rose by `base_amount_in`, and the quote vault fell by `quote_amount_out_without_lp_fee`.

The events reported two fee schedules: **21 sells at 2/93/30 bps** (LP/protocol/creator; 125 bps total) and **eight at 20/5/95 bps** (120 bps total). This confirms that using only PumpSwap global static LP/protocol fields would miss the observed creator and dynamic fees. It also shows the fee tier can differ among observed transactions; MC-019's later one-bank first-tier read must not be backdated. Observed whole-transaction network fees ranged from 5,000 to 1,005,000 lamports (median 55,001) and compute use from 83,761 to 374,576 units (median 98,863). These are successful selected traders' costs, not a Rocket inclusion distribution.

The user base-token pre/post balance was available for 28 of 29 sells and agreed with its event amount in all 28. **No selected user quote-token account had a comparable pre/post pair** in transaction metadata; routed or temporary WSOL account handling prevents an independent user-cash delta here. The event's `user_quote_amount_out` and pool vault movements are protocol evidence, but achieved transferable net cash for a hypothetical Rocket position remains unmeasured. No order was constructed, signed, simulated or sent, and no entry rank or `ENTER` decision changes.
