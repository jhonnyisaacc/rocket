# MC-006 frozen design — unchanged flow-risk replication with lower collector overhead

Parent MC-005. Freeze before this new cohort. MC-005's five-second net buy-minus-sell rank had lower modeled curve-exit unavailability in both splits, but its economic mean was outlier-dependent and its capture had episodic long receipt delays. The falsifier here is whether the **same** risk association appears in another prospective window with measured arrival age. This is a replication of an exit-risk component, not a new entry threshold search.

Acquire one new 600-second, at most 512 MiB Pump log session with capture manifest schema v2. The collector preserves every frame and SHA-256 but syncs the raw file every 64 frames or after 250 ms on append, with a final sync, to reduce callback stalls. Reconcile every fully observed interior-slot signature against `getSignaturesForAddress` on an independent RPC host. Require zero Pump event decode errors, native non-Mayhem state failures and material quote failures. Preserve raw frames, provider pages, failed responses and all cohort exclusions.

Reuse MC-005's native-SOL/non-Mayhem population, t0 as CreateEvent frame receipt, five-second feature checkpoint, net buy-minus-sell count and signature tie break, 0.01 SOL size, t0+7s entry, t0+67s curve exit, 2% adverse slippage on each leg, chronological 70/30 split, 155,000 and 1,000,000 lamport per-leg network scenarios, and explicit zero-recovery stress for a quoteable entry with unavailable curve exit. Do not adjust these from MC-005. Run the same `memecoin_baseline.py` and `memecoin_flow_baseline.py` implementation. The first/last slots and names without the full outcome window remain excluded by the existing gate.

Primary replication metric: exit-unavailable rate among **entry-known** top-quartile names versus the remaining entry-known names in development and untouched evaluation. Require at least 30 entry-known evaluation names, lower top risk than rest in both splits, and no protocol/coverage failures to retain the risk component as an out-of-sample candidate. Report counts, risk difference and exact binomial uncertainty; a zero observed risk count is not proof of zero risk. Report the economic stress means/medians and leave-largest-winner-out top means under both fee scenarios as secondary checks; do not promote a trading rule without stable positive results and transaction-level execution evidence.

Measure CreateEvent event-timestamp-to-receipt gap across the full cohort and by minute. Because the event timestamp is integer seconds, this is an imprecise age proxy, not exact network latency. A p90 above five seconds fails the **fast-discovery** gate for this capture even if the risk association replicates. Record the complete lag distribution and do not remove delayed names after outcomes are known. A lower lag after the sync change is suggestive infrastructure evidence, not proof that disk sync alone caused MC-005's burst.

Retain `TradeEvent.user` and report, as a diagnostic only, the number of distinct event user addresses among first-five-second buys per launch. This address is not automatically a verified buyer or beneficial owner. Do not fit, filter or rank by this diagnostic in MC-006. Any wallet-quality or cycling claim requires a separate transaction-accounting test on a predeclared sample.
