# MC-015 frozen design — signed early-buyer size dispersion

Parent MC-013/MC-014. MC-014 established that signed token-account owner truth for the first three early buys could arrive by five seconds in one short covered window. Its distinct-owner count exactly reproduced the capped buy count there. This new, **untouched 600-second cohort** tests a different mechanism: broadly distributed early buyer expenditure may reduce dependence on one large buyer and lower later curve-exit risk, with possible costed return value. This is an association test, not beneficial-controller or causal proof.

## Acquisition and point-in-time feature

Freeze one 600-second Solana Labs Pump `confirmed` log capture, maximum 512 MiB raw, with two concurrent publicnode read-only companions. The curve companion reads every emitted bonding-curve account at creation receipt +7 and +67 seconds, maximum 32 MiB responses and 720 seconds, as in MC-013. The signed-buyer companion selects the first **three distinct successful buy transaction signatures** received from each native non-Mayhem creation through receipt +5 seconds, maximum 1,500 selected signatures, 32 MiB responses and 720 seconds. It fetches each selected `getTransaction` as soon as the event arrives, at most eight concurrent requests, at most three attempts with 0.5/1-second retry delays. It saves event SOL size as well as mint, token amount, emitted user, signature, slot, source hash, event receipt, request and response clocks, full response and errors. Later responses remain saved but unavailable at the five-second decision. Every creation, excluded regime, zero-buy name, failed retrieval and ambiguous owner remains in the universe.

Require an independent publicnode signature-index audit of valid interior stream signatures with MC-010's structural quarantine rule: zero missing/extra valid signatures, successful-log truncation, decoder failures, native state continuity failures or material quote failures. Both companions must parse the entire raw segment, agree with the independently decoded create/event identities and save every scheduled read. Direct curve accounts must have the Pump owner and pinned layout; stale context, late response, missing fee state or completed/depleted route remains explicit. A signed buy is feature-usable only if its successful transaction has matching signature and slot, a unique event-sized positive token-account owner, and the saved successful response was received no later than that creation's receipt +5 seconds. Preserve signer, payer, route and emitted user separately; distinct owners are not assumed to be independent beneficial controllers. Later strict top-level signed creation identity is a retrospective universe check and cannot be backdated as a feature.

For a native non-Mayhem creation with full 67-second covered logs and at least one selected buy, require **all** of its selected first-three buys to pass that timely signed-owner screen; otherwise the new score is `UNKNOWN`, not zero. Aggregate each verified token owner's selected `TradeEvent.sol_amount` (integer lamports) across those buys. If total buy SOL is positive, let share `p_i` be each owner's spend divided by the selected total, and `k` be the number of unique owners. The frozen score is `0` for `k=1`, otherwise `(1 - sum(p_i^2)) / (1 - 1/k)`, in `[0,1]`. Higher means more even spend *conditional on the selected owners*. It uses no transaction field retrieved after the checkpoint, no future trades and no outcome-based threshold. The complete-case rate and the distribution of `k` must be reported; a score-known subset is never presented as the full creation universe.

## Outcome, comparison and promotion gate

Use the unchanged five-second net buy-minus-sell event score as a control. Preserve all baseline universe rows and split eligible launches 70/30 chronologically by creation receipt, signature tie break, **before** ranking. On the same score-known subset in each split, rank the dispersion score descending (signature tie break) and separately rank frozen net flow descending. For each rank, report top quartile and rest, ties, unknown entry, completed/depleted curve exits and quoteable exits. The account-based model uses a 10,000,000-lamport buy at receipt +7 seconds and curve sell at +67 seconds, 200-bps slippage each leg, and 155,000/1,000,000-lamport network-fee scenarios per leg. Use only timely direct account states and fee parameters visible by the corresponding response; mark stale/unsupported/missing fee state `UNKNOWN`. Curve-exit-unavailable entries receive zero recovery **only in a named stress scenario**; actual inventory recovery and other routes remain unknown. Report quoted-only returns separately. Do not relabel a failed entry as a loss. Report leave-best-out evaluation top stress means and direct-vs-event-state discrepancies.

The minimum **economic candidate** gate requires: at least 30 evaluation names with known entry stress outcomes; at least eight evaluation top-quartile known entries; dispersion top stress mean strictly positive in development and evaluation under **both** fee scenarios; evaluation leave-best-out top stress mean strictly positive under both; and dispersion top stress mean strictly above the frozen flow top on the identical score-known subset under both fees in evaluation. Any missing datum fails its applicable gate. Lower exit unavailability alone does not pass. If these gates fail, reject this specific score as an economic entry rule under the frozen curve proxy without tuning its formula or cutoff on this cohort. Even if they pass, do not emit `ENTER`: actual Rocket inclusion, priority-fee choice, response-to-execution delay, beneficial funding control and contemporaneous alternate-route size-aware liquidation are not measured. A favorable result would only justify a separately frozen execution/funding replication on a fresh panel.
