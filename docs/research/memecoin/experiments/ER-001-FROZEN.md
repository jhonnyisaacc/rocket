## Exact question and cheap falsifier

Does the presence, by a realistic five-minute entry checkpoint, of **at least two historically credible early wallets with no strong observed common-control links** raise the conditional probability of a size-aware, reachable ≥20x outcome by at least threefold and two percentage points relative to matched launch-period controls, with leakage-safe histories and the same frozen effect direction on an untouched temporal slice?

The 20x outcome is primary. 10x, 50x and 100x are secondary descriptive endpoints. A pass earns a proposal for ER-002 replication, never ENTER. An inadequate source earns no favorable result and no graph/watch infrastructure. Do not require ordinary trading frequency; a candidate every 1–3 months or less is acceptable. A population-wide denominator is necessary to estimate that frequency.

## Checkpoints and reachable labels

Reconstruct creation, +30s, +60s, +120s, +300s, migration and subsequent seven-day path. Primary checkpoint is +300s, secondary descriptive checkpoints +30/+60/+120s with the same lag rule. Market-cap crossings at $10k/$50k/$100k are descriptive only when point-in-time supply and quote/USD conversion are reliable, never reconstructed from a later cap.

Fix **$100 total risk** inclusive of modeled costs/rent, converted with contemporaneous SOL/USD. Entry requires a successful route and size-aware historical reserve/fee quote available by the target. Use the first reconstructable bank/trade state at or after action time within ten seconds, with actual source interval recorded; otherwise ENTRY_UNKNOWN, or ENTRY_UNAVAILABLE if verified no valid route. Do not interpolate a candle low or use historical minimum. Buy/sell charge versioned protocol fees, 2% adverse slippage per leg and both 155k/1m lamport network-fee scenarios; these are modeled sensitivities, not Rocket achieved fees. Unknown protocol versions/fees/supply remain unknown.

For hypothetical acquired inventory, `reachable_k` means a sell quote for the full size reaches k × total starting risk, **net of all modeled costs**, at least twice separated by ≥30 seconds within the next seven days. Require timestamped route depth and continuous interval evidence; a single candle high or isolated print is insufficient. Define maximum reachable multiple from these repeated size-aware quotes. Failed exit retains inventory; missing route coverage is EXIT_UNKNOWN, never automatic zero. Record first threshold time, liquidatable depth, path drawdown and completion/migration with distinct clocks. Low-to-peak, leader fill and a 15-minute candle high cannot label this target.

If only candles/trade prices exist, report `price_tail_proxy` in a distinct feasibility/discovery appendix; the primary reachability gate is **unmeasurable** and cannot pass. Migration without usable pool depth is not an exit. Record known negatives, known positives, unknown entry/exit and end censoring separately. Report lower/upper base-rate bounds treating unknowns adversely/favorably, and signal/control missingness. No silent complete-case promotion.

## Wallet histories and actor uncertainty

Work backward from winners only for explanatory diagnostics after primary sampling/features are locked. Build exactly the same histories for all sampled/control-token buyers. Signed buys require token-owner and cashflow/instruction attribution; receipt transfers alone do not count. Never import vendor smart-wallet tags, lifetime PnL or full-period clusters as PIT features.

At each current-token wallet entry timestamp t, use a trailing 90-day prior-token history. Prior entry must precede t and its seven-day outcome horizon must **finish before t minus observation lag**. Filter funding/transfer edges by availability before t. Tokens in progress do not contribute future hits. Store a maximum contributing event/outcome timestamp and assert it precedes t. A wallet profile generated after t may reconstruct earlier facts only with these bounds and explicit historical mode. Later ER-001 token performance must never score its own earlier buyers.

Freeze credible wallet definition: ≥20 previously completed, signed, early (token age ≤300s) prior token entries; ≥3 prior reachable-20x hits; and Beta(1,19) posterior mean `(hits+1)/(n+20)` ≥0.10. Count misses and verified catastrophic exposure in denominator, with incomplete prior histories making credibility UNKNOWN. This is a discovery convention, not a tuned elite label. Record 10x/20x/50x/100x hit counts, posterior shrinkage, sample size, misses, rug exposure, median entry age/percentile/cap, trustworthy realized cash PnL, holding duration and recency. PnL is descriptive, not the selection target. Minimum history is a feasibility gate, not permission to relax the threshold until wallets qualify.

Relations: `KNOWN_SAME_CONTROL` requires explicit signed common-controller evidence; `PROBABLE_SHARED_CONTROL` includes a shared non-service funder financing at least half both wallets' pre-entry native balance within seven days or repeated co-funding on ≥3 prior launches; `POSSIBLE_LINK` includes one shared funding edge, creator transfer or prior direct transfer without stronger evidence; `NO_OBSERVED_LINK` means no evidence in the bounded observed histories, **not proven independence**. Shared exchange/service funders are ambiguous possible links, not known control. Unknown funder classification/history remains UNKNOWN. Freeze seven-day direct funding and one upstream hop; no expanding graph until a favorable effect appears.

Primary confluence excludes pairs with known/probable shared control and requires observed funding history for both; possible links are retained and disclosed. Sensitivity excludes possible links too. Merge known/probable controller groups before counting. Prior co-occurrence uses only completed prior launches and is disclosed as a coordination clue. No beneficial-ownership claims from address distinction.

## Evaluation and frozen stop

Report n, qualifying frequency/month, known/unknown entry and exit, tail counts/rates, 95% Wilson intervals, matched risk ratio and absolute risk difference. Use a launch-day block bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 1001) for 95% intervals, keeping wallet/actor reuse disclosed; additionally leave out each month and each dominant recurring actor. No “independent wallets” sample-size inflation. A point estimate without interval/sample disclosure cannot pass.

A conservative screening pass requires **all**:

- ≥100 ≥2 candidates in discovery and ≥50 in holdout, ≥10 known reachable-20x candidate outcomes overall and ≥5 in holdout; ≥80% primary label coverage in both contrast groups and missingness difference ≤5 percentage points.
- ≥3× matched-control reachable-20x risk ratio and ≥2 percentage-point absolute lift in discovery **and** holdout under both fee scenarios; day-bootstrap 95% lower risk-ratio bound >1 in holdout.
- Same favorable direction under +30s action lag, exclusion of POSSIBLE_LINK pairs, and leave-one-month/leave-dominant-actor checks. Outcome labels and contributing wallet histories pass causal-clock assertions. Report worst-case unknown bounds; if they permit reversal, classify inconclusive rather than promotion.

These effect sizes and minimums reflect a high hurdle for costly infrastructure, not final economic acceptance. Sparse candidates below minimum mean **INCONCLUSIVE / stop escalation**, not proof of no rare alpha. Trivial lift, temporal collapse, leakage or concentrated single-actor dependence mean stop this exact mechanism. Do not tune wallet qualifications, confluence counts, entry clocks or targets on these results. Any materially revised mechanism requires its own direction checkpoint.

Also report loss frequency, verified catastrophic loss frequency, full-position maximum drawdown and tail distribution under fixed-risk bets. A reachable maximum is not an exit policy: do not present mean maxima as tradable expectancy. Give explicit scenario EV for each fixed k: `p_k*(k-1) - (1-p_k)` in risk units assuming timely full liquidation at k and complete loss otherwise, with probability bounds. This optimistic threshold scenario omits real stop/exit decisions and must stay separate from actual cashflow. ER-002 must freeze and validate an implementable exit policy.

If all gates pass, propose ER-002 on materially independent later periods/protocol regimes with untouched data, causally available actor scores, size-aware execution and frozen exit behavior. ## Prospective amendment v1.1 — independent review, before outcome inspection

The initial v1 text is retained in ER-001-FROZEN-v1.md. This amendment fixes estimator/coverage ambiguities; thresholds, primary endpoint, wallet qualification, lag and split are unchanged. Approval covers internal source semantics, dataset acquisition, bounded historical reconstruction and deterministic analysis within ER-001; they do not consume separate experiment budgets.

- **Matching estimator:** match with replacement across candidate sets; each candidate has equal weight. Each of its m controls receives 1/m of that candidate's weight. Candidate rate is the equal-weight mean, control rate the mean of those per-candidate control rates. Report reuse and unmatched candidates separately. Launch-day bootstrap resamples whole matched candidate sets, retaining their controls and weights; reuse/actor dependence is disclosed. If control risk is zero, the risk-ratio point estimate is undefined/infinite, and no pass is permitted without a finite lower-bound estimate supported by data.
- **Unknown exposure:** only wallets whose prior-history and bounded funding coverage are complete can contribute a known actor group. No credible wallets with insufficient history are not proven noncredible. If unresolved buyer credibility/relationship could change the cell, exposure is UNKNOWN, never a 0/1 control. For incomplete launches give lower/upper plausible group counts and an explicit UNKNOWN stratum. No unknown buyer is treated as an ordinary buyer. Primary features end at +295s; entries +296..300s cannot contribute.
- **Label coverage:** known negatives require a complete ordered, timestamped state-changing transaction sequence for all verified usable routes throughout the seven-day horizon, with protocol/fee compatibility and source completeness certified. Positive labels require two valid full-position quotes at least 30s apart and complete compatible route-state coverage over the intervening interval. Missing route state or uncertified trade coverage gives UNKNOWN, not negative. No isolated high print can certify a label. Noncanonical route uncertainty is retained rather than asserting canonical route coverage describes every venue.
- **Prior history:** one distinct signed early token entry per wallet/token, never duplicate buys. Credibility uses the same fee-specific five-minute $100 size-aware token outcome as the current token, with completed seven-day horizon and all contributing observable evidence no later than current wallet entry t−5s. Candidate token is excluded from its own wallet history. A future acquisition timestamp may support explicitly retrospective reconstruction but never becomes a live receipt timestamp. Missing prior outcomes can change n/hits and therefore make credibility UNKNOWN.
- **Interpretation:** cap/liquidity matching conditions on state observed after early actor entries. The estimand is a conditional predictive association, not the causal effect of smart-wallet buying.

Any insufficient source may undergo a bounded semantic/coverage diagnostic using its actual interval. Such records are separate from the primary March–August corpus and cannot pass the primary gate or contaminate the holdout.
